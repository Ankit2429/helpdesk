# Ph.D
 **Source URL:** https://www.kletech.ac.in/research/our-programs/phd
 #### Ph.D. Programs at Research Centers: A Gateway to Knowledge-Driven Growth
 In today's globalized world, knowledge-driven growth is imperative. Our doctoral programs
 are
 dedicated to fostering research that aligns with international standards, empowering students to
 make significant contributions to science, technology, and society.
 Ph.D. Fellowships: To support and attract top research
 talent, KLE Tech offers three categories of Ph.D. fellowships:
 - Dr. Prabhakar Kore Ph.D. Fellowship - Rs. 25, 000/month
- Vice-Chancellor Ph.D. Fellowship - Rs. 15, 000/month
- KLE Tech Ph.D. Fellowship - Rs. 10, 000/month
 #### Ph.D. Opportunities across Departments and Centers: Our doctoral programs span a wide array of
 disciplines:
 - Civil Engineering
- Computer Science and Engineering
- Electronics and Communication Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Management Studies and Research
- Automation & Robotics
- Biotechnology
- Electrical and Electronics Engineering
- Chemistry
- Mathematics
- Physics
- Engineering Education & Research
 At KLE Tech, research is a journey of exploration, learning, and societal impact. With a focus on
 innovation and interdisciplinary collaboration, we strive to create a research culture that nurtures
 the spirit of inquiry and equips our students to address some of the world's most pressing
 challenges.
