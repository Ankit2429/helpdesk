 # Center for Energy and Environment (CEE)
 Center for Energy and Environment (CEE) was established in 2021
 and is dedicated to promoting sustainable
 energy solutions and environmental applications. With a multidisciplinary team of experts, it
 conducts
 innovative research, develops practical applications, and provides policy recommendations to address
 the
 pressing challenges of energy efficiency and environmental issues. Through collaborations, the
 center strives
 to accelerate the transition towards a clean and renewable energy future.
