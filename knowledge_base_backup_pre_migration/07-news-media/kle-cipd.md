 #### AboutCIPD
 Centre for Innovation and Product Development (CIPD)
                                    Established under the guardianship of KLE Technological University. The center focuses on Product Innovation, Design, development and Technology Transfer of KLE Tech IP's for MSMEs (Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises). CIPD shall develop and guide start-ups to become entrepreneurial ventures.
 Legal counselling & advisory services about IPRs, IP Protection, IPR filing and infringement of IP rights.
 Provides Design Training, Design Services, Prototype Development and Validation facilities.
 Effective marketing resources both online and offline to reach target customers.
 Ensures overall growth of a start-up into an established business venture.
