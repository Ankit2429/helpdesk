 What We Offer
 The Central Library KLE Technological University provides all reading and learning resources and state-of-the-art infrastructural facilities.
 ### Library Collections
 The Central Library has an Excellent Collection of Resources like Books, E-Books, E-Journals, Databases, CDS/DVD's, Architectural Designs, Rare Books, Bound volumes. Competitive Books like GATE, KPSC, UPSC, Banking Services, Engineering Services, NDA and General Books like Novels, Story Books Etc.., along with the Pulitzer Award-Winning Books, Booker Prizes Winner Books, Alumni & Faculty Books, Video Streaming Lectures. The number of hard copies of National and International Journals, Magazines, Weekly,
                           Fortnightly, Monthly is regularly subscribing.
 ### Library Space
 The Central Library has sufficient. Book Stack Area, Reference Area, Quadrangular Reading Space, Journals Reading section, Discussion Lounges and Open Spaces Reading facilities across Campus provided.
 ### Infrastructural Facilities
 The central library has a very. Good Ambience with State of Art infrastructural facilities designed with Good Atmospheric condition with Natural Air and Light.
 ## KLE University Central Library
 The KLE Technological University New Central Library came into existence in 2003 with a carpet area of 3000 Sq. Mtrs are having state of the art infrastructure facilities with 600 seating capacity.
                   The Central Library has a collection of 95337 volumes of Books with 39400 Titles. The Central Library is also subscribed to 181200 ebooks from EBSCO Academic Collection with EBSCO Business Elite sources.
                   The Central Library collects ebooks from McGraw hill Access Engineering, Pearson ebooks, PHI India, New Age International, McGraw hill Express, Science Direct, Springer, CBS, and VIDEEYA ebooks perpetual access.
                   The Central subscribe 65 National and International printed Journals predominantly related to Engineering, Technology, Management and allied subjects.
