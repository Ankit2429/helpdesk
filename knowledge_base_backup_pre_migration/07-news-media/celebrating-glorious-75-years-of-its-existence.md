 ### Nearby Location
 Hubballi (Hubballi), or Pubballi ("Old Village"), developed around the 11th-century stone temple of Aharanishankar.
 Notable buildings include the Mahadi Mosque, the Bhavani Shankar Temple, and the city hall. Hubballi is a trading centre with cotton mills, ginning and pressing factories, and a large newspaper industry.
