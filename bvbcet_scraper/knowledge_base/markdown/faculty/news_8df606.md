## Feb 24, 2026


### Idaho State University has appointed Nagaraj Shetti as an Affiliate Faculty Member

Idaho State University has appointed Nagaraj Shetti as an Affiliate Faculty member in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering for the 2025–2028 academic term.

In a letter dated February 24, 2026, Provost and Vice President for Academic Affairs Adam Bradford, PhD, confirmed the appointment following strong endorsements from Dr. Bruce Savage, Chair of the Political Science Program, and Dr. Jim Widmann, Dean of the College of Science and Engineering. The appointment recognizes Shetti’s credentials, proposed contributions, and continued engagement with the university community.

The university expressed its appreciation for his contributions, noting that he is a valued asset to both the institution and the broader community.
