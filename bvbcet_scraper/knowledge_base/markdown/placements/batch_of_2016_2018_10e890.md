### Sector-Wise Information

The sector-wise split for the various functions is provided below. Marketing & Sales, and Operations contributed the equal percentage of offers (24%) and HR (19%). On-campus and Off-campus together 20 firms had participated for 2016-2018 campus recruitment. The new recruiters for this year include like Vistaar Financial Service Pvt Ltd., Ninjacart, Joules to Watts, D-Mart, Anzy Careers, Kotak Mahindra Bank, Pepperfry and Robert Bosch

The highest annual salary was Rs. 5.50 lakhs p.a. Average salary was Rs. 2.70 lakhs p.a
