- Careers

Every employee is hired to make significant contributions towards KLE
Technological University.

Our faculty members are accomplished academicians, researchers, and agents of
social transformation who
share the University’s mission of leveraging knowledge to build
the nation and improve the quality of
life in this region.

Apart from having the requisite qualifications as prescribed by the Board of
Higher Education, our faculty
has a significant body of research work that has resulted in journal and
books with reputed publishing
house.


#### Many of our faculty are practicing professionals,
with proven field experience.

This broad spectrum allows us to offer several
interdisciplinary courses as part of the
curriculum.

We are looking for academicians and industry
practitioners with a passion for teaching, to
join us as
faculty members.

Hiring for Faculty and Non-Faculty positions at KLE
Technological University follows the
“Service Rules” and the Board of
Higher Education guidelines.


#### For Faculty positions, it is expected that the
pre-requisite qualifications are in line with
the
AICTE norms.

Along with Teaching Skills, it is desirable that the
candidate has a proven track of research or
Industry experience.

At KLE Technological University, Educational Merit,
Cultural Ethos, Inclusivity, and Integrity are
the baseline of the hiring process, and
Excellence in Teaching, Research, and Service
are the key
drivers of Career growth.

We expect and encourage every faculty member to set
goals and contribute fully to the
University’s Mission.

We invite you to be part of this beautiful journey
towards creating Knowledge and Leveraging it to
build a better society.
