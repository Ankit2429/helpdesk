Chancellor KLE Technological University Chairman, KLE Society, Belagavi


#### Dear Students,

Seeing you in KLE Tech is a matter of pride to me. In a very short period KLE Tech has etched a place of eminence in the field of education in India and is recognized globally. I am sure you will find the learning experience extremely rewarding and enriching.

You are the future of the engineering and technological excellence of India and the global community. The value we nurture in KLE Tech is ensuring that our students and faculty are an integral part of the worldwide society. We believe in the age-old ethos of “Vasudaiva Kutumbakam” (the whole world is one family). I want to emphasize that for the last few decades, KLE Society has worked hard to bring together great minds, allowing a free flow of knowledge to build a better world.

I dream of seeing our young students excel in their chosen fields and contribute significantly to world-building. I am immensely proud to share with you that our illustrious alumni are already doing yeomen service in their respective areas of specialization. I want you to join this sparkling constellation.

When we transformed B.V. Bhoomaraddi College to KLE Technological University, it was with a vision to make the institution a center of excellence and a significant center of learning in architecture, engineering, and technology, in northern Karnataka. I am happy to share that our faculty and administrative staff have strived to make this institution among the best.

Last year, we were recognized by none other than the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) as “One of the Five Global Institutions That Are Places to Watch in Engineering Education.” As we advance, we are working towards making KLE Technological University multi-campus and multidisciplinary research-intensive University. I welcome you to KLE Tech and wish you a bright future and the most satisfying career in your life ahead.
