### Introduction

The National Cadet Corps (NCC) at KLE Technological University (KLE Tech) is pivotal in nurturing discipline, leadership, and patriotism among students. Aligned with the university's commitment to holistic education, the NCC unit offers cadets a platform to develop essential life skills and serve the nation.


### Objectives of NCC at KLE Tech

- Character Building: Instill discipline, responsibility, and ethical values in cadets.
- Leadership Development: Foster leadership qualities and decision-making abilities.
- Patriotic Spirit: Cultivate a deep patriotism and commitment to national service.
- Physical Fitness: Promote physical well-being through regular drills and activities.
- Community Engagement: Encourage participation in social service and community development initiatives.


### Activities and Initiatives

- Military Training: Regular drills, weapon training, and camps to impart military skills.
- Adventure Programs: Trekking, mountaineering, and other adventure activities to build resilience.
- Social Service: Blood donation drives, Swachh Bharat campaigns, and awareness programs on health and hygiene.
- National Events: Participation in Republic Day, Independence Day parades, and NCC Day celebrations.


### Achievements

The NCC cadets of KLE Tech have consistently demonstrated excellence:

- B Certificate Examination: Cadets have successfully undertaken the 'B' Certificate examination, marking a significant step towards their NCC journey.
