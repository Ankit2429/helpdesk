## Nov 11, 2023


### Alumni Spotlight

We are incredibly proud to share the extraordinary achievements of our alumnus, Altaf Ganihar, from the E & C Branch, Class of 2015. He's not only the CEO of Snaptrude, but he's also a trailblazer in the architecture, engineering, and construction (AEC) design sector!

Founded in 2017 at KLE Tech Park and now headquartered in New York, Snaptrude is on a mission to disrupt the AEC industry, challenging giants like Autodesk, Trimble, and Nemetschek with its cutting-edge, cloud-based approach. Their vision is taking shape with a groundbreaking $14 million raised in a Series A round, just 10 months after announcing their seed funding.

This remarkable journey wouldn't have been possible without the guidance of Dr. Uma Mudenagudi, who played a crucial role in shaping Snaptrude's success story.

For all the details and insights into this incredible milestone, check out the TechCrunch article

Join us in congratulating Altaf Ganihar and the entire Snaptrude team on their remarkable success! Let's cheer them on as they continue to reshape the AEC industry with their innovation and determination.
