Sub: Conduct of II &amp; IV semester examinations of 3 Year LL.B. (Fresher's Only) reg...

Ref: Hon'ble Vice-Chancellor approval dated : 02-02-2022

The Hon'ble High Court of Karnataka, Dharwad Bench in W.P. No. 104008/2021 dated : 14-12-2021 passed an order directing the Karnataka State Law University to promote the II and IV Semester fresher students of 3 Year LL.B. to the next semester. The University preferred Writ Appeal W.A. No. 100319 of 2021 the Hon'ble Divisional Bench HighCourt of Karnataka Dharwad Bench has stayed the said order pending the proceedings.

In view of the above, II &amp; IV semester examinations of 3 year LL.B. (fresher's only) are scheduled from 17-02-2022. The detailed time-table will be issued at the earliest. Kindly bring it to the knowledge of concerned students and staff. It is also to clarify that the mandatory notice period of 60 days is already complied as the examinations were notified earlier on 17-09-2021.
