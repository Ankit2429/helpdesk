## CIRCULAR

Sub: Re-Scheduling the date of Commencement of Classes for "Even" semester for the academic year 2022-23.

- Ref: 1)KSLU/Reg/Acad-II/Acad.Calendar/2022-23/1327Dated: 04.11.2022.

- 2) Approval of Hon’ble Vice-Chancellor, Dated: 04.05.2023.

With reference to the above, it is herey informed that all the Principals of Affiliated Law Colleges of Karnataka State Law Univesity, that the reopening and commencement of the classes for "Even" semester for the year 2022-23 was scheduled from 06.05.2023. However due to the continuous participation of teachers for valuation work and for the completion of Valuation work within reasonable time. The commencement of the classes is rescheduled from 15.05.2023. Further, the Colleges are directed to conduct extra classes on holidays to compensate the extended period. However it is to inform that there will not be changes in the schedule stipulated in academic calendar referred at 1.

Further it is directed that this information shall be brought to the notice of all the teachers and students.
