# Center of Intelligent Mobility (CIM)

- Home
- Center of Intelligent Mobility (CIM)

The Centre for Intelligent Mobility (CIM) at KLE Technological University is
dedicated to shaping the
future of transportation to ensure safer, efficient, and eco-friendly mobility. Its goal is to
facilitate
technological advancement and spearhead research initiatives within the domains of perception and
control (ADAS/AD), Communication, and Security. ADAS /AD technologies form the backbone of
safer driving experiences, and the center focus is on leveraging AI/ML algorithms to assist drivers
in
navigating traffic, avoiding collisions, and managing various road conditions in real-world
scenarios.
Security is of paramount concern in the realm of intelligent mobility. Connectivity lies at the
heart of
modern transportation, and our center is at the forefront of integrating vehicles into a connected
ecosystem. Research collaboration with industries like, NVIDIA, LTTS and BOSCH has elevated the
research profile of the CIM.
