# Center for Automation & Robotics Research (CARR)

- Home
- Center for Automation & Robotics Research (CARR)

The Center for Automation & Robotics Research (CARR) is committed to advancing
the field of mechanical
engineering by bridging the gap between academia and industry. This facility is the epicenter of
this vision,
designed to facilitate groundbreaking research and skill development. CARR provides a platform for
the
faculty to pursue advanced research in smart manufacturing, digital twin and human-robot
collaboration,
and a multitude of related technologies. At the same time, it empowers our students with hands-on
experience and practical skills in cutting-edge technologies. It aims to foster partnerships with
industry
leaders, ensuring our research and students are at the forefront of industrial innovation.
