## Oct 07, 2023


### Felicitation ceremony honors 21 distinguished scientists and engineers of ISRO who are also our alumni

KLE Technological University - Hubballi (India) proudly celebrated the accomplishments of 21 exceptional alumni who have made remarkable contributions to the ISRO - Indian Space Research Organization. In a prestigious felicitation ceremony held on 7th October 2023 at Bio Technology Auditorium, the outstanding achievements of these scientists and engineers were acknowledged, coinciding with the auspicious occasion of World Space Week.

The event was presided over by, Dr. Prabhakar Kore, Chairman, KLE Society Dr. Ashok Shettar, Vice Chancellor, KLE Technological University - Hubballi (India), Shri. Shankaranna Munavalli, Director, KLE Society, Dr Basavaraj Anami, Registrar, Dr. Prakash Tewari, Dean Academics, Prof. B. L. Desai, Executive Dean, Deans, HOD's, Principals and students were present on the occasion.

The honorees, who have played pivotal roles in India's space endeavors, shared their experiences and insights during the event, inspiring the current students of KLE Technological University - Hubballi (India) to aim for the stars and reach new heights in the field of space technology.

Dr. Prabhakar Kore, Chairman of the KLE Society, emphasized the importance of recognizing the achievements of these distinguished alumni, stating, "Our alma mater has been a cradle of talent, and these 21 alumni have brought great honor to our institution. They exemplify what can be achieved with determination, dedication, and the right education."

Dr. Ashok Shettar, Vice Chancellor of KLE Technological University, commended the BVBCET alumni, saying, "ISRO's accomplishments have placed India on the global space map. These 21 individuals have contributed significantly to ISRO's success, and their journey serves as an inspiration to the current generation of students who aspire to excel in the field of space technology."

The felicitation ceremony was a momentous occasion for KLE Technological University - Hubballi (India), which takes pride in its long-standing tradition of nurturing talent and promoting excellence in engineering and technology.

The event was marked by speeches, presentations, and interactions with the ISRO alumni, giving attendees valuable insights into the world of space technology and exploration. It also served as a reminder of the importance of World Space Week in promoting science and technology for peaceful purposes and celebrating the benefits of space for humanity.

KLE Technological University - Hubballi (India) looks forward to continuing its tradition of excellence and producing more talented engineers and scientists who will contribute to India's space endeavors and technological advancements in the future.
