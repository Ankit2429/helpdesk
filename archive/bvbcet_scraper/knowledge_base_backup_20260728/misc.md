## BVBCET : Home

Source: https://www.bvb.edu/

The B.V. Bhoomaraddi College of Engineering & Technology in Hubli was founded in 1947. It is one of the best engineering colleges of India. It started as a Polytechnic in Gadag but was moved to Hubli in 1948 and simultaneously upgraded to an Engineering College.

## BVBCET :

Source: https://www.bvb.edu/achievements.html

|   ### RARE QUALITIES RARE  REWARDS  At BVBCET, great emphasis is  placed on student projects. This enables students to define objectives, analyze  requirements and apply the knowledge they have gained to such projects, which  see industry involvement and often funding too by agencies such as KSCST, are  opportunities for students to explore the limits of their abilities. The  excellence fostered and nurtured by the college has resulted in a host of  awards. These include:
                The Championship Award (Technical) at SRISHTI  2003 held at PESIT, Bangalore and  the runners up award at SRISHTI 2004 at SJCE, Mysore.  Organised annually by ABVP, SRISHTI is a state level Techno Management Fair  that judges the quality of student projects.
                “Best College  of the Year, 2002” at KSCST Student Project Programme held at SIT, Tumkur. This  event, organized by the Karnataka State Council for Science and Technology  annually is an exhibition of all student project funded by it.
                First prize at the National Projects  Presentation Competition held at APS College of Engineering, Bangalore  in June 2003, a team consisting of Anand Baid, Shankar Joshi, Lalit Daya and  Darshan Banavi presented the project “Cable TV Automation System” to win the  prize.
                Award winning project at 34th All  India Student Competition, 2003 organized by the National Design and Research  Forum of the Institution of Engineers (India),  Kolkata. The project “Software Teaching Aid for Artificial Neural Networks,  ANN-IT” was presented by Chetan Kotturshettar and Vinayak Bhat. ###   |
