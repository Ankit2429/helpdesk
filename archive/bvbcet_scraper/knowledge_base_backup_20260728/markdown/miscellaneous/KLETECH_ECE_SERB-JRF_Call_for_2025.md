  ADVERTISEMENT FOR THE POST OF JUNIOR RESEARCH FELLOW (JRF) 
  Applications are invited from eligible Indian nationals for the post of Junior Research Fellow (JRF) in a time 
bound SERB -POWER: Science and Engineering Research Board - Promoting Opportunities for Women in 
Exploratory Research, sponsored project undertaken at School of Electronics and Communication Engineering 
of the University. The details are as follows. 
  Project Title e-KRUSHI: PRECISION AGRICULTURE 
Funding Agency SERB-POWER: Science and Engineering Research Board - Promoting 
Opportunities for Women in Exploratory Research, Govt. of India 
Temporary Positions Junior Research Fellow (JRF) - 01 
Consolidated monthly Fellowship Rs. 31, 000 per month 
Investigators Dr. Suneeta V. Budihal and Dr. Saroja V. Siddamal 
Department/Centre School of Electronics and Communication Engineering 
Duration Over all 03 years 
Qualification and Experience Qualification: 
 M.E/M.Tech in E&C / IT / Computer Science or in related 
disciplines with consistently good academic record. 
 Candidates must have at least 60% (CGPA 6.5/10) marks in 
aggregate from a recognized technical institute or university. 
 Candidate should have qualified GATE at least once in his/her 
academic career. 
Experience: 
 The candidate should have good knowledge of machine learning 
and image processing concepts. Experience in implementing 
deep learning-based projects is an added skill. 
 Good communication and writing skills for technical 
report/ article. 
More information Selected candidates are encouraged to register for PhD at KLE 
Technological University, Hubballi, provided the candidate completes 
the academic and administrative requirements. 
  Interested candidates are invited to email their detailed CVs in th e prescribed format, along with scanned 
copies of self -attested certificates, grade/mark sheets, publications, etc., to careers@kletech.ac.in and 
suneeta_vb@kletech.ac.in. For more details, visit: www.kletech.ac.in/career on or before March 30, 2023. 
  The shortlisted candidates will be informed by E-mail/Phone. Selection will be based on qualification, 
experience, and performance in the interview. KLE Technological University, Hubballi , reserves the right to 
reject any or all the applications without assigning any reasons thereof.
