 ### KLE Technological University to Host Prestigious KLETech Republic Day Cup 2026
 Hubballi, 19 January 2026: KLE Technological University will host the much-awaited KLETech Republic Day Cup 2026, a FIDE-rated national chess tournament featuring both Rapid and Blitz formats, on January 25-26 at the state-of-the-art Dr. Prabhakar Kore Sports Arena on the BVB Campus in Vidyanagar, Hubballi.
 Hubballi, 19 January 2026: KLE Technological University will host the much-awaited KLETech Republic Day Cup 2026, a FIDE-rated national chess tournament featuring both Rapid and Blitz formats, on January 25-26 at the state-of-the-art Dr. Prabhakar Kore Sports Arena on the BVB Campus in Vidyanagar, Hubballi.
 Organized under the aegis of Dharwad District Chess Association, Karnataka State Chess Association (KSCA), and All India Chess Federation (AICF), the event will attract top players from across India, including Grand Master Sri Thejkumar M. S., Secretary of KSCA.
 Inauguration and Chief Guest
 The tournament will be inaugurated by Honorable Vice-Chancellor Dr. Prakash G. Tewari on the morning of January 25. The prize distribution on January 26 evening will be graced by Dr. M. P. Baligar, Vice-Chancellor of Karnataka State Akkamahadevi Women's University.
 Attractive Prize Pool
 A total Rs. 5, 00, 000 prize fund awaits winners:
 - Rs. 4, 00, 000 for Rapid format
- Rs. 1, 00, 000 for Blitz format
 Cash prizes, trophies, and medals will be awarded across rating-based, age, and open categories.
 Event Partners and Schedule
 Coordinated by the university's Gymkhana and Physical Education Department, with Vachan Chess Academy, Hubballi as organizers. The two-day event starts with the opening ceremony on January 25 and ends with prize distribution on January 26.
