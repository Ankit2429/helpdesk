# B.Sc. in Hotel Management and Catering Technology (B.Sc. HMCT)
 **Source URL:** https://www.kletech.ac.in/coursedetail.aspx?mpgid=30&pgidtrail=30&courseid=91
 ## B.Sc. in Hotel Management and Catering Technology (B.Sc. HMCT) is a three-year undergraduate programme commencing in 2026,
 designed to prepare students for leadership, culinary, and management roles in the global hospitality sector. The curriculum integrates theoretical business studies with intensive, hands-on practical training across core hotel operations.
 #### Core Areas of Study
 The syllabus is highly experiential, delivered through simulated five-star environments and commercial kitchen settings. Primary subjects include:
 - Food Production - Culinary arts, baking, and advanced kitchen management.
- Food & Beverage Service - Restaurant operations, bar management, and event catering.
- Front Office Operations - Guest relations, reservations, and property management systems.
- Housekeeping & Accommodation Management - Hotel maintenance, interior aesthetics, and facility planning.
- Business & Management - Financial management, tourism marketing, food safety, and human resources.
 #### Career Scope & Opportunities
 Graduates are equipped for diverse, high-growth careers across the global hospitality industry. Typical roles include:
 - Management Trainee - Hotel chains, luxury resorts, and international hospitality brands.
- Executive Chef / Catering Manager - Large-scale events, industrial catering, and flight kitchens.
- Front Office Executive / Guest Relations Manager - Luxury hotels and premium service sectors.
- Hospitality Entrepreneur or Consultant - Independent ventures and advisory roles.
 - Eligibility & Admissions
Candidates must have completed 10+2 (Senior Secondary) or equivalent from a recognised board, with English as a core subject and a minimum of 50% aggregate marks in Science or Commerce.
