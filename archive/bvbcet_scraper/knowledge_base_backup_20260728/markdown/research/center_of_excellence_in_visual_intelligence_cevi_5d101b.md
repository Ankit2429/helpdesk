 # Center of Excellence in Visual Intelligence (CEVI)
 The Centre of Excellence in Visual Intelligence (CEVI) at KLE Technological
 University focuses on developing
 cutting-edge AI solutions to address real-world challenges. It specializes in visual intelligence
 research for
 both 2D and 3D data, spanning tasks like image restoration, synthetic data generation,
 classification,
 detection, tracking, scene understanding and multimodal data analysis across domains such as Digital
 Heritage, Agriculture, and Smart Manufacturing. CEVI also integrates Extended Reality (XR) for
 immersive
 research presentations and fosters impactful academia-industry collaborations. Through its
 partnership with
 Samsung R&D Institute, Bangalore, CEVI established the Student Ecosystem for Engineered Data
 (SEED)
 lab,
 creating a vibrant ecosystem for students to innovate in the field of visual intelligence.
