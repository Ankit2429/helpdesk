 ### Celebration of the Silver Jubilee by the BVB Alumni of the 1998 Batch!
 What a wonderful celebration of the Silver Jubilee by the BVB alumni of the 1998 batch! ?? It's heartening to hear that nearly 120 alumni from various parts of India and even the USA gathered on the campus for this special occasion.
 A big shoutout to the organizing team led by Mr. Shivanand Havanagi, along with Mr. Shiva Arali, Mr. Kiran Jambhekar, Mr. Naveen Hemadri, and Mr. Rahul Kocheri for putting together such a successful event.
 The event's program sounds both enriching and nostalgic, starting with the annual report presentation by the Alumni Association's Secretary and the Guru Vandana program, which honored the dedicated teachers and staff who played a pivotal role in the alumni's educational journey.
 Hon'ble Vice Chancellor, Dr. Ashok Shettar's address, highlighting the evolution of BVB College of Engineering & Technology into a State Private University, was undoubtedly inspiring. His call for alumni to support the current generation of students in their academic endeavors is a testament to the institution's commitment to excellence.
 It's also impressive to note the shift towards Outcome Based Education for Teaching Learning Process, as explained by Dr. Prakash Tewari, Dean Academics. This approach ensures that students receive a well-rounded and effective education.
 The alumni's commitment to supporting their Alma Mater is heartwarming and speaks volumes about the strong bond that exists among the BVB alumni community.
