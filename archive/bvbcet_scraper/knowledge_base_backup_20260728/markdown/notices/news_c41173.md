 ### CMDR Ideathon 2026
 KLE Technological University's EEE students of 4th semester Bhumi R Hanehalli, Neha Jambanagoud, Tejas Chandaragi and Anusha Patil secured Second Prize & won Cash Prize of Rs. 7, 000 in CMDR Ideathon-2026.
 The Ideathon was organized by the Centre for Multi-Disciplinary Development Research (CMDR), Dharwad, on 26th February 2026.
 And The topic of the event was "Innovate for Dharwad: Implications of Dividing HDMC as an Independent Dharwad Municipal Corporation".
