 #### UG & PG Programs of Higher Semesters Only
 We are pleased to announce that from 2020-21 academic year admissions to all higher semesters of UG & PG Programs shall be compulsorily online only. Online admissions shall be through the website: https://parents.kletech.ac.in Student only if he/she is eligible to next academic year must log on to the above website after the announcement of results to generate the Fees Bill.
 - Student has option to pay online fees payment in State Bank of India only.
- Second Year full fees payment has to be done through parents.kletech.ac.in/ only.
- Once you click online payment, you have following options to pay.
 - SBI Online Payment: Net Banking from Your Account (SBI or Any Other Bank)
     Card Payments : SBI Debit Card/ Other Bank Debit cards/ Credit Cards
- Other Payment Mode : By cash only after downloading SBI Challan
- Students who makes payment thru online State Bank of India, the payment status is updated immediately & student can generate admission form through kletech.ac.in
- Student who makes payment thru SBI offline, the payment status will be updated in 2-3 days time. Student need to check the status of payment credited to College account on parents.bvb.edu site before generating admission.
- Student who makes payments thru Syndicate Bank offline, shall submit the copy of the challan to accounts office for updating the payment. After this student shall download admission form through bvb.edu
