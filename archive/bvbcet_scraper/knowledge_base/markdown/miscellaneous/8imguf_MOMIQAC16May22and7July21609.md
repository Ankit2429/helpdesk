Coordinator, IQAC welcomed all the members present and requested Vice Chancellor to chair the
meeting. He briefed the members present on the agenda. The salient points of the discussion are
as follows;
1.    NAAC peerteam visit:
The NAAC peer team visited KLETECH during 7-9th March 2022 for university assessment.
During  the   3-days  visit  the  team   conducted   qualitative  assessment  of  criteria's  and
parameters as per NAAC guidelines with respective stakeholders. On the end of 3rd day a
formal  report/feedback  was  shared  to  all  stakeholders,  at  the  closing  meeting  held  in
Biotechnology Auditorium.
2.    NAAc accreditation rating and analysis of NAAC score:
The university received  ``A" grade with  a score of 3.10  in the first cycle, declared  on  15th
March  2022,  which  is  valid  till  14th  March  2027.  A  detailed  analysis  of  score  for  both
qualitative and quantitative matrices was presented  by Dr.  P.G. Tewari,  Director,  IQAC to
all the members. Chairman,  lQAC congratulated the efforts of all team  members, faculty,
students and staff for obtaining "A" grade. Further Chairman,  lQAC suggested to make an
action plan for improving university overall performance in sub-sequent AQAR 2021-22 to
be submitted to NAAC post first cycle of accreditation.
3.    Re-Constitution of lQAC cell:
ln view of few administrative changes and requirement it was decided to re-constitute the
composition   of   lQAC.   The   chairman   advised   the   .Director,   IQAC   to   nominate   the
composition as per NAAC / AQAR guidelines.
The meeting was concluded with a formal vote of thanks by lQAC coordinator.
