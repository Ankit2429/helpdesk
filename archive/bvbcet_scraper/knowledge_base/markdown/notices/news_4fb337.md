## Mar 08, 2024


### Celebrating a Moment of Pride!

Heartiest congratulations to our esteemed alumna, Dr. Sudha Murty, on her nomination as a Rajya Sabha Member by Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi!

Dr. Murty, a beacon of inspiration and a distinguished graduate of BV Bhoomaraddi Engineering College (now KLE Technological University), has always exemplified excellence in literature, philanthropy, and education.

Her journey from our halls to the hallowed chambers of the Rajya Sabha is a testament to her unwavering commitment to societal progress. We couldn't be prouder to see her represent the ideals and values instilled at BV Bhoomaraddi Engineering College.

As KLE Tech University, we celebrate this moment with immense joy and pride. Dr. Sudha Murty's achievements resonate with our commitment to nurturing leaders who drive positive change in the world.

Here's to a trailblazer, a leader, and an inspiration! May your journey in the Rajya Sabha be as remarkable as the impact you've made in every endeavor. Best wishes for this new chapter!
