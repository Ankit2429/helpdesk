### KLE Technological University Inaugurates IEEE PELS Student Branch Chapter

KLE Technological University, Hubballi, took a significant step forward in fostering professional and technical engagement among its students with the inauguration of the IEEE Power Electronics Society (PELS) Student Branch Chapter on 4th May 2026. The Chapter, formally approved by the IEEE Power Electronics Society, is hosted by the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering and aims to build a strong foundation for student participation in cutting-edge research, industry collaboration, and professional development in the domain of power electronics.

Inaugural Ceremony

The event commenced with an invocation by Ms. Hrutika Saboji, followed by a warm welcome address by Dr. Sujata N. Patil, who introduced the distinguished gathering to the vision and global activities of IEEE PELS. The formal inauguration was marked by the traditional lighting of the lamp by the dignitaries on the dais.

The ceremony was graced by Dr. Prema V., Chair, IEEE PELS Bangalore Chapter, as Chief Guest, and Smt. Chaitanya L., Vice Chair, IEEE PELS Bangalore Chapter, as Guest of Honour. Dr. Prema V. spoke on the expanding role of power electronics in transformative technologies such as electric vehicles, renewable energy systems, smart grids, and sustainable mobility, urging students to actively engage with IEEE's research and collaborative platforms. Smt. Chaitanya L. emphasized how membership in professional societies equips students with critical skills in networking, leadership, and industry readiness.

Chapter Activities and Office Bearers

Dr. Vinod S. Patil, Staff Advisor of the IEEE PELS Student Branch Chapter, outlined a robust activity plan for the academic year 2025–26, encompassing technical workshops, expert lectures, industry interactions, technical competitions, and collaborative research initiatives. The newly elected office bearers of the Chapter were formally introduced to the gathering, and badges were presented to mark the occasion.

Leadership Addresses

The programme was presided over by Dr. (Smt.) Nalini C. Iyer, Dean – Faculty of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, who commended the Department's initiative and motivated students to harness this platform for technical growth and innovation. Dr. Saroja V. Siddamal, Head of the Department, expressed strong institutional support for professional engagement through IEEE activities.

Sri Amit Kore, Chairman, KLE Society, extended his best wishes and lauded the Department for establishing the Chapter. Hon'ble Pro-Chancellor Dr. Ashok S. Shettar and Hon'ble Vice-Chancellor Dr. Prakash G. Tewari inspired students to actively participate in technical and professional activities, urging them to make the most of platforms like IEEE to build their competence and leadership potential.

The vote of thanks was proposed by Mr. Amit Vikram, Chair, IEEE PELS Student Branch Chapter, and the event was compered by Mr. Anmol Patil.

Technical Session: "Fault Quest"

Following the inauguration, students participated in an engaging technical session titled "Fault Quest" — a structured challenge designed to sharpen problem-solving skills, analytical thinking, and practical understanding of electrical and electronic systems. The session drew participation from more than 30 teams representing various disciplines, reflecting the strong enthusiasm among students for applied and competitive learning.
