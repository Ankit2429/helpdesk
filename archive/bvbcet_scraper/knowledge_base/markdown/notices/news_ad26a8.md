### The 6th National Constitutional Law Moot Court Competition 2023-24 at KLE Law College, Bengaluru

Exciting Announcement! Join us for the 6th National Constitutional Law Moot Court Competition 2023-24 at KLE Law College, Bengaluru!

Dates: November 24-26, 2023

	Inauguration Ceremony - November 24th, 4:30 PM

	Venue: Auditorium, KLE Law College, Bengaluru

	Chief Guest: A. V. Chandrashekara, Former Judge, High Court of Karnataka

Guests of Honour:

	- Sri. P. S. Rajagopal, Senior Advocate, High Court of Karnataka

	- Prof. (Dr.) Khushal Vibhute, Distinguished Visiting Professor

	Symbiosis Law School, NUJS Kolkata & MNLU Aurangabad, Former Professor & Dean, Law School, IIT Kharagpur

Witness thought-provoking discussions and insights from legal luminaries! Don't miss out!

Valedictory Ceremony - November 26th, 4:15 PM

	Venue: Auditorium, KLE Law College, Bengaluru

Chief Guest:

	Hon’ble Mr. Justice Sreenivas Harish Kumar, Judge, High Court of Karnataka

Guests of Honour:

	- Hon’ble Mr. Justice G. Basavaraja, Judge, High Court of Karnataka

	- Hon’ble Mr. Justice Venkatesh Naik T., Judge, High Court of Karnataka
