 ### International Youth Biodiversity Conference
 KLE Technological University's Biotechnology scholar, Pratiksha K, secured the 1st Prize & Funding at International Youth Biodiversity Conference organized by Telangana Biodiversity Board Govt of Telangana.
 Pratiksha with her guide Dr Zabin Bagewadi with their performance made a mark at the International Youth Biodiversity Conference thereby bringing honor to KLE Technological University.
