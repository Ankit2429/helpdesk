 ### Blood Donation Drive!
 The blood donation camp held on KLE Society's 108th Foundation Day was an incredible success! The collaborative effort of the Community Development Circle, NSS, KIMS, and Rasthrothana Blood Bank made it possible and truly inspiring.
 Dean, Academics, Dr. Prakash Tewari's touching words about the Saptarshis' sacrifice and the importance of blood donation deeply resonated with everyone. Encouraging regular donations every three months is a remarkable step towards fostering commitment to this noble cause.
 Dr. Purushotam Reddy's emphasis on the World Health Organization (WHO)'s slogan "Donate Blood, Donate Plasma, Save Life, Save Often" shed light not just on blood donation but also on the life-saving impact of plasma donation. It's a reminder of how these contributions can truly make a difference.
 The presence of Dr. Sanjay Kotabagi, Dean, Student Welfare, Dr. Veeresh Angadi, Principal Munivalli Polytechnic College, Prof. M.H. Kuragodi, Prof. Vijaykumar, Prof. Minal Salunke and others added immense value to the event, highlighting the significance of our collective efforts.
 With an overwhelming collection of over 275 units of blood, our community's generosity and support shone brightly!
 These events play a vital role in raising awareness and meeting the ongoing need for blood donations. Thank you all for being a part of this incredible initiative!
