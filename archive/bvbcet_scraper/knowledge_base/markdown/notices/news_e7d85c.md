 ### Samsung Chip Design Studio Contest
 Students from KLE Technological University's B E Electronics Engineering, VLSI Design & Technology, secured 1st place in Samsung Chip Design Studio contest.

 - Shashidhar kumbar 8th sem
- Pratiksha kulkarni 8th sem
