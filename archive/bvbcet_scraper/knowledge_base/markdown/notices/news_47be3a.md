 ### Arun Kambrekar Dominates State-Level Powerlifting Championship, Clinches Gold
 In a display of sheer dominance and unwavering determination, Arun Kambrekar, a student from KLE Tech University, has emerged triumphant at the State-level powerlifting championship held in Nelamangala, Bengaluru.
 Arun's exceptional performance in the fiercely contested 66kg weight category has garnered widespread admiration and applause. Securing the top position was no mean feat, underscoring Arun's dedication and relentless pursuit of excellence.
 Here's a glimpse of Arun's extraordinary lifts:
 Squat: 235kg
 Bench Press: 110kg
 Deadlift: 225kg
 With an impressive total of 570kgs, Arun clinched the gold medal, showcasing not only his physical prowess but also his mental fortitude and resilience.
 The entire KLE Tech University community extends heartfelt congratulations to Arun for this remarkable achievement. His victory serves as a shining example of commitment, perseverance, and the pursuit of greatness.
 As Arun embarks on his future endeavors, we have no doubt that he will continue to scale greater heights and inspire others with his passion and dedication. Arun, your journey is a source of inspiration for us all.
 Best wishes and continued success from all of us at KLE Tech University!
