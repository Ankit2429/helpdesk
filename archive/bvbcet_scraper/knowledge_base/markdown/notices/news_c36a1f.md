 ### KLE Technological University's team wins 1st prize at hackathon at PESITM
 THE 'Beyond Minus' team from KLE Technological University won the top prize of Rs 1 lakh at the 'Hack Malenadu-2026' hackathon, a 24-hour national event that concluded at the PES Institute of Technology and Management (PESITM) on Saturday.
 The hackathon, held on Friday and Saturday, successfully brought together approximately 600 higher education students and professionals from across India to develop innovative solutions for real-world challenges.
 PESITM stated in a press release that the event saw an unprecedented response, attracting 2, 356 registrations from 729 teams across cities, including Bengaluru, Chennai, and Mysuru. 150 teams, representing 60 engineering colleges nationwide, were selected for the final round. Participants engaged in continuous coding, ideation, and brainstorming sessions, guided by 25 mentors.
 The 'Beyond Minus' team from KLE Technological University, consisting of Vraj Joisav, Sumanth K S, and Sreejith Mundra won the first prize of Rs 1 lakh. Abhiram, Sanjana N Patel, and Namrata K B from GM University won the second prize of Rs 50, 000. The 'Logic Weavers' team from RNS Institute of Technology, comprising Nissehay Sai D R, Rishika J R, and Noti Gayathri won the third prize of Rs 25, 000.
 A major highlight of the event was the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Jnanik AI, ZySk Technologies, and Elevana Consulting to strengthen industry-academia collaboration and create new opportunities for students.
 The hackathon received support from sponsors and technology partners, including Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), Jnanik AI, and Magure. Key attendees at the closing ceremony included Varun V, founder of ZySk Technologies; Maneer, business head of Hack Culture and Sudhanshu Shekhar, founder of Elevana Consulting. Officials from PESITM, including principal Dr Swami D R and vice-principal Dr Sekhar R, were also present.
