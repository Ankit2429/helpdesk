 ### KLE Technological University hosts Entreva-2025 Inauguration Ceremony
 Hubballi: The Department of Commerce at KLE Technological University hosted the inauguration of the state-level inter-collegiate competition, Entreva 2025, with great enthusiasm and participation.
 60 different teams from all across Karnataka participated in the event, showcasing the vibrant entrepreneurial spirit amongst the students.
 Mr. Mrunal Bankapur, an Administrator, Strategist, and Development Head of BSC Textiles, South India's largest textile retailer, inaugurated the event. This was followed by his address, where Mr. Bankapur emphasized the essential traits of successful entrepreneurs and outlined the prerequisites for entrepreneurial growth, inspiring the young participants.
 The keynote address was delivered by the Dean of KLE Technological University, Dr. Shollapur, while Mr. Arogyaswamy A. Karadi, Head of the B.Com Department, extended a heartfelt welcome to the gathering through his kind words.
 Dr. Guruvarya Hiremath, Head of MBA, and Dr. Suvarna, Head of BBA, also graced the event with their presence and lent their support to the initiative.
 In essence, events like Entreva 2025 act as catalysts in establishing leadership and entrepreneurial skills among students, further strengthening the university's commitment to holistic education and industry engagement.
