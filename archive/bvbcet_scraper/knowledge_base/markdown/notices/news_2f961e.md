### KLE Technological University Triumphs Once More at SAE SUPRA 2024!

We are thrilled to share that our talented team has achieved remarkable success at the prestigious SAE SUPRA 2024, securing an All India Rank of 5th overall in the event! The competition took place at the Buddha International Circuit, Delhi, from October 2nd to 5th.

The team's exceptional performance included: 1st Position in Autocross

2nd Position in Acceleration With over 50 teams participating, our students' hard work, dedication, and innovative spirit have truly made us proud. This outstanding achievement is a testament to their perseverance and excellence. We congratulate the team and look forward to more such milestones!
