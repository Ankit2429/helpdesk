 ### 9th International Day of Yoga was celebrated at KLE Technological University.
 The 9th International Day of Yoga was celebrated at KLE Technological University in Hubli today with great fervour and participation. The event focused on the theme of "One World - One Family, Yoga for the Good of All." Esteemed yoga trainers led participants through various yoga asanas, emphasizing the importance of physical and mental resilience in facing challenges like pollution and COVID-19.
 The event was attended by the KLE Technological University Vice-Chancellor Dr Ashok Shettar, Dr Sanjay Kotabagi, Prof. Vijayakumar, Prof. M. M. Kuragodi, Principal Viresh Angadi (Polytech), Prof. Desai, Prof. Sanagowdar, Prof. Chikkerur, Prof. Murali, Prof. Uma Mudenagundi, Prof. Mr Mallikarjun Marihala, Mr Jayadev, Dr Puranik, Mrs Patil, and Mrs Sushila were present promoting the holistic well-being of the university community. Participants left with a renewed commitment to incorporating yoga into their daily lives.
