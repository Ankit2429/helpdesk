 ### Victorious Skies: Team AeroKLE Triumphs in Autonomous Flight at SAEINDIA Aerothon 2023!
 We are thrilled to announce that aeroKLE SAE Aero-Design Team has soared to new heights and emerged victorious in the field of Autonomous Flight at the SAEINDIA Aerothon 2023!
 The team's exceptional skills and innovation were on full display as they adeptly utilized MATLAB and Simulink for UAV simulations, video calibration, and precise aerial data annotation. A special shout-out to Team AeroKLE for integrating a Fuzzy PID controller, showcasing their prowess in optimal control techniques and emphasizing excellence in autonomous aeronautical engineering.
 Your dedication, hard work, and commitment to pushing the boundaries of autonomous flight have truly set a benchmark in the industry. May this success be the first of many achievements to come for Team AeroKLE! Congratulations once again on this remarkable accomplishment, and may your future endeavors continue to reach new heights!
