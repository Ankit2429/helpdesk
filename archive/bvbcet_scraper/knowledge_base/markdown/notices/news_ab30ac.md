 ### The Future of HR - Powered by AI
 KLE Technology is thrilled to have partnered with National Institute of Personnel Management - (NIPM) India North Karnataka Chapter
 "The Future of HR - Powered by AI" hosted by School of Management Studies and Research - KLE Technological University was indeed North Karnataka's first major HR-AI convergence, bringing together 200+ HR leaders, students, faculty, and industry professionals to explore transformative technologies in L&D, Talent Acquisition, Performance Management, and Employee Experience.
 Proud highlights from our SMSR team Involve:
 Distinguished Speakers: Mr. Anjan A & Mr. Lalit Suresh delivered actionable insights on AI-driven HR evolution
 Exceptional Moderation: Mr.Veerabhadra Patil (Co-Founder & COO, Skillemme) masterfully guided dynamic discussions
 Leadership Support: Special thanks to Mr. Ramchandra Joshi , Mr. Bheemachand Rathod, Dr. Dr. Narayana Poliki, and NIPM NKC team for seamless coordination
 Our heartfelt appreciation to SMSR faculty - Dr. M R Shollapur, Dr. Dr. G S Hiremath, Dr. Dr. Rajshekhar Navalgi- for driving this initiative that positions Hubballi as an emerging hub for future-ready HR capabilities.
 This collaboration exemplifies KLE Tech's commitment to bridging academia-industry gaps through cutting-edge programs. Excited for more such initiatives that empower North Karnataka's workforce for the AI era!
