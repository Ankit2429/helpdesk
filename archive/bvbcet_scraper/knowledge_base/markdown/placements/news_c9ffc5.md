### Defence Career Expo

Akshay G Athreyas, 8th sem EEE Student of KLE Technological University secured a 2nd Place in Triquest at the Defence Career Expo, organized by Kumaraguru Institution of Technology.

Triquest is conducted on the pattern of the SSB process with multiple elimination rounds coordinated by the three armed forces.

KLE Tech congratulates Akshay G Athreyas, on his extraordinary performance and for making KLE TECH proud.
