# News
## Nov 11, 2023
### AIU South West Zone Taekwondo competition
That's fantastic news! Congratulations to Shivani Patel (LL.B., @klelawcollegeblr) for her impressive achievement in the AIU South West Zone Taekwondo competition. Qualifying to represent your university in the All India Inter University Taekwondo competition is a remarkable accomplishment, and it reflects her dedication and hard work. It's great to see individuals like Shivani making KLE Technological University proud through their achievements in sports.
Special recognition goes to Laxmi Narasappa, the Physical Education Director, and Master Parappa, Shivani's coach at @jjtuniversity, for their valuable support and guidance in helping Shivani reach this level of success. Teamwork and mentorship play a crucial role in the journey of any athlete, and it's wonderful to see that Shivani had a strong support system.
Wishing Shivani all the best in the upcoming All India Inter University Taekwondo competition in January 2024. May she continue to shine and make her university proud on the national stage!
