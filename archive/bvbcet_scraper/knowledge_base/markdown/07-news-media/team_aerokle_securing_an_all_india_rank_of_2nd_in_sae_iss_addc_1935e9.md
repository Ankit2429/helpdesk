# Events
## Feb 02, 2026
### Team AeroKLE securing an All India Rank of 2nd in SAE ISS ADDC
KLE Technological University’s Team AeroKLE recently participated in the event SAE ISS ADDC organized by SAE INDIA Southern Section.
The problem statement of the event was to autonomously deliver a fragile payload to a given waypoint location.
AeroKLE delivered the payload with an accuracy of 15cm from the given waypoint while bagging the All India Rank 2 as they won a cash prize of Rs. 50,000
Additionally, AeroKLE was also awarded the best presentation award with a cash prize worth Rs. 5000.
