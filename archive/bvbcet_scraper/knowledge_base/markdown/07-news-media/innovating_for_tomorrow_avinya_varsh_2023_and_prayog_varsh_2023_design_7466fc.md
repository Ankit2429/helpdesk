# News
## Jul 20, 2023
### Innovating for Tomorrow: Avinya Varsh 2023 and Prayog Varsh 2023 - Design Thinking and Engineering Exploration Exhibitions
The Avinya Varsh 2023 and Prayog Varsh 2023 Exhibition, held on July 20, 2023, at the Dr. Prabhakar Kore Sports Arena, was a remarkable showcase of innovative projects developed by students enrolled in the "Design Thinking for Social Innovation" and “Engineering Exploration” courses. This event provided a platform for these students to present their creative solutions to real-world problems, with the goal of encouraging social innovation and exploration. The exhibition attracted a substantial number of visitors, reviewers, and esteemed guests, making it a resounding success.
The inauguration of the event by the esteemed Vice-Chancellor, Dr. Ashok Shettar, set an inspiring tone for the exhibition. His presence and motivating speech encouraged the students to continue their efforts in making a positive impact on society through innovation. A compendium was released at the exhibition, serving as a document to preserve the event's details. This compendium featured comprehensive information about each project, including the problems they addressed, the innovative solutions proposed, and the teams behind the projects. The exhibition showcased a diverse array of projects that tackled various social issues, ranging from sustainability and healthcare to education. These projects were the result of weeks of rigorous brainstorming, ideation, and prototyping by the students, highlighting their dedication and creativity.
In total, 261 projects were exhibited during the event, drawing approximately 2500 visitors. The audience included students, faculty, industry professionals, parents, and members of the public. This overwhelming response reflected a growing interest in design thinking and social innovation. Around 30 reviewers from KLETU and other institutions were invited to evaluate the projects and provide valuable feedback. These reviewers, hailing from diverse backgrounds, brought their expertise and insights to ensure a comprehensive evaluation of each project's potential impact and feasibility.
The projects on display received widespread appreciation from both reviewers and visitors. The students' dedication to finding sustainable solutions to pressing social problems was commendable, and their work garnered praise for its relevance and impact potential.
In conclusion, the Avinya Varsh 2023 and Prayog Varsh 2023 Exhibition proved to be an exceptional showcase of creative talent and social consciousness. The event provided a valuable platform for students to display their design thinking capabilities and their dedication to creating meaningful social innovations. The success of the exhibition underscored the importance of integrating design thinking into social projects and its potential to bring about positive change in the world. It also highlighted the students' ability to innovate and their commitment to applying engineering principles to address real-world challenges effectively.
As the event concluded, participants and visitors left with a sense of inspiration, hope, and a renewed commitment to contribute to a better and more sustainable future through design thinking for social innovation.
