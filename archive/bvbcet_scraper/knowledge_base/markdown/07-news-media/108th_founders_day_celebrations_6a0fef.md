# News
## Nov 13, 2023
### 108th Founder's Day Celebrations
KLE Technological University marked the celebration of the Founders Day of The Karnataka Lingayat Education KLE Society on 13th November 2023, a significant event to commemorate the vision and establishment of the society. The occasion served as a platform to pay tribute to the founders and acknowledge their invaluable contributions to education and society at large.
The ceremony witnessed the active participation from the university, including Dr. Prakash Tewari, Dean Academics, Dr. B. B. Kotturshettar, Dean & HOD, Dr. Uma Mudenagudi, Dean R & D, Dr. Sanjay Kotabagi, Dean, Student Welfare, Dr. Bhavikatti, Dr. M R Patil, Ms. Minal Salunke, Prof. Sanagoudar, and numerous others.
The presence of Heads, Deans, Faculty and staff added a sense of unity and shared purpose to the celebration. Their collective presence underscored the importance of acknowledging and preserving the rich heritage and values upon which the KLE Technological University stands.
In conclusion, the Founders Day celebration at KLE Technological University was a fitting tribute to the visionaries behind the institution. It brought together the academic community to express gratitude, reflect on achievements, and reinforce the commitment to the principles that have shaped the university's identity. The event served as a reminder that the legacy of the founders continues to inspire and guide the university towards a future of excellence and societal impact.
