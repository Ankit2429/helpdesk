# News
## Dec 20, 2025
### Munde Banni
KLE Technological University hosted the Mundhe Banni Meetup at the Campus in Hubballi! This vibrant gathering brought together founders, aspiring entrepreneurs, students, and industry leaders from North Karnataka to spark innovation and ecosystem growth.
Key Highlights from the Day:
RiNK Initiative Launch – Our Pro Chancellor Dr. Ashok Shettar officially launched Rise in North Karnataka – a game-changing program to bring tech talent home and enable companies to thrive in our region. Professionals and startups, register now: risenk.com and Live Better, Build Bigger from Namma Ooru!
VC Insights Panel – Vinod Shankar (Java Capital), Nihal Shetty (Rainmatter by Zerodha), and Vasant Shetty (Mundhe Banni Founder) shared real-talk on fundraising, investor expectations, and building scalable ventures from Tier-2 cities.
Branding Masterclass – Raffic Aslam (ex-Zoho) delivered actionable strategies for startups to craft global brands with local roots – no big budgets required!
KLE Technology University acknowledges the Mundhe Banni team for their vision in bridging industry expertise with our local talent pool.
