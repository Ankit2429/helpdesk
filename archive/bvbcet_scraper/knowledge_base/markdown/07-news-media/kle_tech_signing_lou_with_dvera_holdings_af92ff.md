# News
## Nov 12, 2025
### KLE Tech signing LoU with Dvera Holdings
On 12th November, 2025, KLE Technological University took a step towards social impact by signing a Letter of Understanding with Dvera Holdings. Dvera Holdings, which is a mission-driven organization aims at advancing financial inclusion through innovation and tech-driven solution. And so far they have enabled over USD 26 billion in finance thereby impacting more than 125 million lives across India.
This partnership focuses on supporting Innovative research and knowledge sharing with the help of finance, technology, and social impact, especially in the sect of rural wealth management, agriculture, dairy, healthcare, and the gig economy. This collaboration in the representation of a common vision to create a Strong, sustainable and inclusive ecosystem which provide financial strength of economically backward societies.
