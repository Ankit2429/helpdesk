# News
## Mar 27, 2025
### Congratulations to Mr. Mallikarjun M. Yadawad
That's fantastic news! Congratulations to Mr. Mallikarjun M. Yadawad for his outstanding achievements at the 18th National Level Open Karate Championship in Shivamogga. Winning the 1st place in kata (action) and 2nd place in KUMITE (fight) is a remarkable feat, and it's a testament to his dedication and skill in the sport.
It's wonderful to see such talent and determination in the field of martial arts. Mr. Yadawad's success reflects not only his individual capabilities but also the support and training he receives from his coaches, mentors, and the A Z Martial Arts Academy India.
On behalf of KLE Society - Media and KLE Technological University - Hubballi (India) we wish him continued success in his karate journey. Such achievements are truly inspiring and serve as an excellent example to others.
