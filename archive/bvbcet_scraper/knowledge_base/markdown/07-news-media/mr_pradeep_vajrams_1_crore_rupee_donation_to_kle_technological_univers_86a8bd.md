# News
## Jun 20, 2023
### Mr. Pradeep Vajram's 1 Crore Rupee Donation to KLE Technological University.
B.V. Bhoomaraddi Alumnus Mr Pradeep Vajram's 1 Crore Rupee Donation to KLE Technological University Students Welfare Fund shines during Impactful Technical Talk on AI & Semiconductor Industry held on 17th June 2023
Mr. Vajram, handed over the cheque of Rs. One Crore to Dr. Ashok Shettar, Vice Chancellor of KLE Technological University in the presence of Prof. B. L. Desai, Executive Dean, Dr. B. S. Anami, Registrar, Dr. Prakash Tewari, Dean Academics, Shri Ashok Tolannavar, President, BVB Alumni Association, Dr. Uma Mudenagudi, Dean R & D and Dr. Nalin Iyer, Dean.
We express our sincere appreciation to Mr Pradeep Vajram for his invaluable contribution to this event and his dedication to supporting aspiring students. His commitment to philanthropy and entrepreneurial achievements exemplifies the values we strive to instil in our institution's alumni.
Mr. Pradeep Vajram's event on AI's influence on semiconductors at the prestigious BT Auditorium captivated a diverse audience of media, faculty, students, and industry professionals, offering a unique exploration of AI's impact and implications across sectors.
This was a groundbreaking milestone in our institution's commitment to fostering knowledge and innovation in the field of AI and semiconductor technology.
