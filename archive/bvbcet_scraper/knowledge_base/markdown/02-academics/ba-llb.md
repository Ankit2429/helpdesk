# B.A. LL.B
 **Source URL:** https://www.kletech.ac.in/course/ba-llb
 ## The 5-year B.A.LL.B. integrated programme, from 1996, offers a unique opportunity to concurrently earn both a prestigious Arts degree and a reputed Law degree.
 With a successful track record of 26 graduating cohorts, this programme has consistently proven to be the ideal choice for individuals who are keen on making a commitment to the legal profession early in their educational journey, right after the 10+2 stage.
 The programme offers a range of pragmatic courses that empowers students to maintain a competitive edge in excelling in judiciary exams or civil service exams or to brace for challenges posed in the litigation realm or allied avenues.
