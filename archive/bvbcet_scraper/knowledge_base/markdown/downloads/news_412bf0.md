### KLE Team Achieved AIR #2 in Overall Performance in Autonomous Drone Design Challenge

We are excited to inform that our team achieved AIR #2 in overall performance in Autonomous Drone Design Challenge (ADDC) oranginsed by SAEINDIA Southern Section, which took place at KCG College of Technology on 25&26 August 2023. The objective of the challenge was to autonomously transport a fragile payload from one location to another and then return to the initial take-off position. We express our gratitude to the organizers, mentors, and sponsors for their invaluable support. Moving forward, we are enthusiastic about further innovation and exploration of the capabilities of autonomous drones.
