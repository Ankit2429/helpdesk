### KLE Technological University's IIC Shines with 4-Star Rating in SWRO Zone's Innovation Performance 2022-23

KLE Technological University's MOE's Institutions Innovation Cell (IIC) achieving a 4-star ranking in the overall category for the South-West/SWRO Zone in the annual performance assessment for 2022-23 reflects the institution's dedication and success in fostering innovation and entrepreneurship among its students and faculty. This recognition underscores the university's commitment to promoting a culture of innovation and creativity within its campus ecosystem. Achieving such a high ranking in the IIC's assessment is a testament to the institution's efforts in nurturing innovative ideas and initiatives.
