 ### KLE Technological University's IIC Shines with 4-Star Rating in SWRO Zone's Innovation Performance 2022-23

This recognition underscores the university's commitment to promoting a culture of innovation and creativity within its campus ecosystem. Achieving such a high ranking in the IIC's assessment is a testament to the institution's efforts in nurturing innovative ideas and initiatives.
