### The IEEE Conference on Engineering Informatics 2024 (ICEI 2024)

26-28th November 2024, IIT Dharwad, IIIT Dharwad and KLE Technological University

The IEEE Conference on Engineering Informatics 2024 (ICEI2024) is a high-quality scientific meeting that brings together researchers, engineers, and industry professionals to showcase current innovations and technologies in engineering informatics and digital practices across various domains. The conference will feature keynote speakers from eminent personalities from academia and industry, pre-conference workshops, industrial presentation slots, and networking opportunities. ICEI2024 is organized collaboratively by IIT Dharwad, IIIT Dharwad, and KLE Technological University on the 26th - 28th of November 2024 in Dharwad-Hubballi, India. ICEI2024 is a continuation of the conference that will be held at Swinburne University of Technology, Australia, on the 20th and 21st of November, 2024.

ICEI2024 provides a link between computer science, electrical, electronics, mechanical, and civil engineering, covering the latest developments in 5G and 6G, manufacturing, civil, aerospace, robotics, mechatronics, and construction, and providing insights on the effective implementation of new developments.

ICEI2024 includes a wide range of topics from the below key tracks, including.

- Track 1: AI/ML
- Track 2: Industry 4.0
- Track 3: Robotics, Control, and Automation
- Track 4: 5G and 6G Technology
- Track 5: AR/VR and HCI
- Track 6: Critical Infrastructure

ICEI2024 Organizing Committee welcomes original work not previously or not simultaneously submitted to another journal or conference. Individual submissions may include regular papers (6-10 pages). The last date for regular paper submission is 15-Sept-2024. The proceeding of ICEI2024 will be published in the IEEE Xplore, which is indexed in leading databases such as Scopus, etc. Additionally, accepted and invited papers might be submitted to the IEEE Engineering Management Review.

The proceedings of ICEI2023 have been published in IEEE Xplore and can be accessed at https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/xpl/conhome/10520249/proceeding.

For more information, refer to the conference website at https://icei2024.iitdh.ac.in/.
