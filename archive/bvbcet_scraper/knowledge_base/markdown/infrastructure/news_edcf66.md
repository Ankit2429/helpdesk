 ### Star Labs - Received Three Travel Grants to Attend the IEEE
 STAR lab - CEVI is excited to announce that they have received three travel grants to attend the IEEE International Conference on Computer Vision - ICCV-2023 in Paris, France, an A* conference in Computer Vision. For more information about the conference, you can visit the official website: https://lnkd.in/dvydAqeG
 Microsoft Research Travel Grant
  Recipient: Ms. Chaitra Desai (PhD scholar)
  Grant Amount: Rs. 1.05 Lakhs
 Women in Computer Vision Student Travel Grant
  Recipient: Ms. SAMPADA MALAGI(MS Engineering by Research student)
  Grant Amount: 1000 Euros
 Women in Computer Vision Faculty Travel Grant
  Recipient: Dr.Uma Mudenagudi
  Grant Amount: 1125 Euros
 Congratulations to the recipients, and best of luck to STAR lab - CEVI at ICCV 2023!
