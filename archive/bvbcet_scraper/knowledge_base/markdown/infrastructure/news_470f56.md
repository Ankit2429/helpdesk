 ### Outstanding Gymnast Nikhil Badiger Claims Double Silver at Dasara CM Cup 2023
 Congratulations to Nikhil Badiger (BE Civil, 3rd Sem.) for securing two silver medals in Artistic Gymnastics at the Dasara CM Cup 2023 held at Chamundi Vihar Stadium, Mysore. It's an impressive achievement to win silver medals in the Parallel Bars and Roman Rings events. Nikhil Badiger's dedication and talent in gymnastics have clearly paid off in this prestigious competition held from 11th to 14th October.
