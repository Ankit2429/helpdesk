# Center of Excellence in Material Science (CMS)

The Center for Material Science focuses on groundbreaking research, innovation,

and development of novel materials to address critical industrial and societal challenges. With

expertise in materials synthesis, processing, characterization, and computational modeling, the

Centre specializes in polymer, ceramic, and metal-based nanocomposites, biopolymer membranes for

separation and packaging, thin films for tribological and sensing applications, and nanomaterials

for energy storage and harvesting. It conducts multidisciplinary research to drive technological

leadership and economic growth while fostering collaborations with industries and entrepreneurs for

technology transfer and consultancy. The Centre is committed to sustainable development, energy

efficiency and societal challenges ably supported by state-of-the-art facilities. Center contributes

to education, training, and knowledge dissemination, addressing the growing demand for skilled

professionals and positioning itself as a hub for cutting-edge material science research and

innovation.
