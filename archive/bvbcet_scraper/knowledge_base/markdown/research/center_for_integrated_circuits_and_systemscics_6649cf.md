# Center for Integrated Circuits and Systems(CICS)

The Center for Integrated Circuits and Systems (CICS) at KLETECH

envisages to foster innovation and excellence in VLSI Design, Mixed Signal SoC Design and

Verification, DFT and IP Development with a specific emphasis on building a strong vertical in the

domain of Advanced Packaging Analysis/ Design and 2.5D/ 3D System Integration.

The center offers Industry Standard Skilling Programs, Internship/ Projects to the

academia and corporates, engaging industry leaders for training, consultancy, and technical

collaboration.

Strategic partnerships with global EDA leaders like Synopsis and Siemens, the GCCs  AMD, Intel,

Micron, leading service providers (TCS, HCL, L&T), and OSAT houses (Tata Electronics, Kaynes)

form the cornerstone of our initiatives.

By driving innovation, nurturing talent and embracing collaboration, CICS aims to contribute to

shaping the future of the semiconductor design ecosystem in India.
