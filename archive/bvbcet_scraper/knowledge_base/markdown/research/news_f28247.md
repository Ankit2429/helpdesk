 ### Revolutionizing Honey Harvesting: KLE Tech's Patented IAB Device in Indian Innovators Association's 2023 Yearbook!
 KLE Technological University's Centre for Innovation and Product Development (CIPD) proudly presents its patented project, the "Non-Invasive and Non-Intrusive Honey Harvesting Device (IAB)," featured in the esteemed Indian Innovators Association's 2023 yearbook!
 This recognition celebrates our commitment to innovation and marks a significant milestone in revolutionizing honey harvesting techniques.
 A heartfelt thank you to everyone involved!
