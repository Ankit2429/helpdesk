 # EV Innovation Centre
 The Electric Vehicle Innovation Center (EVIC) at KLE Technological University is a
 pioneering hub dedicated to the research and development of next-generation electric vehicle
 technologies. Our center brings together students, faculty, and industry experts to address pressing
 challenges in the EV ecosystem, such as enhancing battery efficiency and integrating EVs with
 renewable energy grids. The center is committed to advancing sustainable mobility by driving
 innovation in electric vehicle technology. Our mission is to lead in research, education, and
 industry collaboration, focusing on battery technology, EV charging infrastructure, and grid
 integration solutions. EVIC is dedicated to creating a cleaner, smarter, and more connected future
 for transportation.
