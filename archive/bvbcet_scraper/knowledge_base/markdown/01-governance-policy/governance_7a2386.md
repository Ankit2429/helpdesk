# Governance and Academic Boards

## KLE Society (Karnatak Lingayat Education Society)

KLE Technological University is promoted by the **Karnatak Lingayat Education Society (KLES)**, Belagavi, Karnataka, which is the university's sponsoring body. The society was founded on **13 November 1916** in Belagavi by seven school teachers (the "Saptarishis"), with the aim of "empowerment through education." Today it runs over 250 institutions (Wikipedia says over 300) in education, healthcare and research, with over 1,30,000 students; the university's KLE Society page cites 13,000 trust members and 16,000 faculty. The current Chairman of its Board of Management is **Dr. Prabhakar B. Kore**.

## Legal Basis

KLE Tech is a State Private University established under the **KLE Technological University Act, 2012 (Karnataka Act No. 22 of 2013)**, at Vidyanagar, Hubballi. Its predecessor, B. V. Bhoomaraddi College of Engineering and Technology (BVBCET), founded by KLE Society in 1947, was upgraded to university status in 2014 or 2015 (the two university documents differ). The First Statutes were approved by the Board of Governors and notified on 25 July 2015.

## Officers of the University

The First Statutes (2015) define the officers as the Chancellor, Pro-Chancellor, Vice-Chancellor, Pro Vice-Chancellors, Deans, Registrar, Finance Officer and Controller of Examinations.

- **Chancellor** – Appointed by the sponsoring body (KLES); Head of the University, whose decision is final and binding on disputes. Current Chancellor: **Dr. Prabhakar B. Kore** (also Chairman, Board of Management, KLE Society, Belagavi).
- **Pro-Chancellor** – Appointed by the sponsoring body in consultation with the Chancellor; assists the Chancellor and acts in his absence. Current Pro-Chancellor: **Dr. Ashok S. Shettar** (a former Vice-Chancellor).
- **Vice-Chancellor** – Appointed by the Chancellor; the Principal Executive and Academic Officer with general supervision over university affairs. Current Vice-Chancellor: **Dr. Prakash G. Tewari**.
- **Registrar** – Appointed by the Chancellor; the Principal Administrative Officer, custodian of assets, minutes and legal documents, and Member Secretary of the Board of Governors. Current Registrar: **Dr. Basavaraj S. Anami**.
- Other officers: up to three Pro Vice-Chancellors (appointed by the Vice-Chancellor with the Chancellor's written approval), Deans (appointed by the Vice-Chancellor with Board approval), the Finance Officer, and the Controller of Examinations.

## Authorities of the University

Under the Act and First Statutes the authorities are the **Board of Governors, Executive Council, Academic Council, Research Council, Finance Council and Estates' Council**.

### Board of Governors (BOG)

The BOG is the **principal governing and policy-making body**, exercising all powers not otherwise provided by the Act and able to review decisions of other authorities. Nominated members hold office for three years (extendable by six months); the quorum is three members. The university's "List of Board of Governors" page shows:

- Dr. Prabhakar B. Kore – Chairman, Board of Management, KLE Society, Belagavi and Chancellor, KLE Tech
- Dr. Ashok S. Shettar – Pro Chancellor, KLE Tech
- Dr. Prakash G. Tewari – Vice Chancellor, KLE Tech
- The Principal Secretary/Secretary, Higher Education, Government of Karnataka
- The Principal Secretary/Secretary, Medical Education, Government of Karnataka
- Dr. Sudha N. Murty – Founder, Infosys Foundation; Chairperson, Murty Trust; Member of Rajya Sabha
- Dr. Madhusudan V. Atre – Co-Founder, AGNIT Semiconductors, Bengaluru (nominee of the sponsoring body, KLE Society)
- Prof. B. L. Desai – Executive Dean, KLE Tech
- Dr. N. H. Ayachit – Dean Academics, KLE Tech
- Dr. Basavaraj S. Anami – Registrar, KLE Tech

### Executive Council

The Executive Council is the **principal executive body** and reports its minutes to the Board. Per the university's Executive Council page, the Vice-Chancellor chairs it; the Pro-Chancellor is advisory member; members include Dr. Madhusudan V. Atre, Dr. Syed Shakeeb Ur Rahman, the Deans of Academics and Planning & Development, and the Executive Dean; the Registrar is Member Secretary.

### Academic Council and Boards of Studies

The **Academic Council** is the principal academic body: it coordinates and supervises academic policy, scrutinizes Board of Studies proposals on courses, curricula and syllabi, and constitutes committees including the Boards of Studies.

**Boards of Studies (BoS)** are constituted at school/department level. The university's BoS notifications show a consistent pattern: the Head of Department/Dean of the school as Chairperson; departmental faculty nominated by the Dean; external subject experts, industry/corporate representatives, a meritorious postgraduate alumnus and student members, largely nominated by the Vice-Chancellor. School-level Boards exist for Automation and Robotics, Computer Science, Civil, Electronics and Communication, Architecture, Mechanical, Electrical and Electronics, and Management Studies and Research.

### Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC)

The IQAC was constituted on **6 July 2018** to plan, guide and monitor quality assurance and enhancement activities and to prepare the Annual Quality Assurance Report (AQAR) for NAAC. Per IQAC meeting minutes, its membership includes the Vice-Chancellor (Chairman), the Registrar, the Director of IQAC (Dean Academic Affairs, Dr. P. G. Tewari), the Deans of Planning & Development, Research & Development and Student Welfare, heads of schools/departments, the Finance Officer, a representative of the Board of Management of KLE Society, and student and alumni representatives. AQAR 2022-23 lists Dr. P. G. Tewari as Director/Coordinator; earlier minutes name Dr. Sanjay Kotabagi as Coordinator.
