# Intenational Admission
 **Source URL:** https://www.kletech.ac.in/hubballi/cpage.aspx?mpgid=178&pgidtrail=190
 ### Overview
 Those seeking admission under NRI quota have to register online, fill an online application form
       and make fee payment through SWIFT/ E- Payment/ NEFT/ RTGS transfer on or before the deadline
       mentioned in the schedule on KLE Tech website. Further, online application and documents should
       be uploaded to KLE Tech website through the portal only. It is important to note that only
       registered applicants will be considered for admission.
 ### Eligibility Criteria
 Following are the eligibility criteria
 Age: Applicants born on or after October 01, 1997 are eligible. Date of birth as recorded in the
       Secondary Education Board / University Certificate [Class X or equivalent] or any certificate
       issued by the Government authorities only will be taken as authentic.
 ### Academic Eligibility
 All fee payments during admission for the first year BE /B Arch Programs to the University shall be made
    online only through the University portal, which shall be supported by Admission office.Alternatively one
    can make fee payment by Bank demand draft in the name of "The Registrar, KLE Technological
    University", payable at Hubballi.
 Applicant must have passed the qualifying examination, i.e. Senior Secondary [10+2] or equivalent [See
    Appendix-I] from any system of education as recognized by the Association of Indian Universities (www.aiu.ac.in) with specified subjects as mentioned below.
 Applicants appearing for the qualifying examination in the academic year 2021- 22 and expecting their final
    results latest by December 31, 2022 may also apply.
 Applicant should have completed successfully Mathematics, Physics and one of the subjects from (Chemistry,
    Bio-technology, Computer Science, Biology) in 11th and OR 12th, as applicable in the respective boards.
 #### Residential Requirement
 Prescribed admission criteria for Indian Nationals shall be at least two years of education,
       inclusive of 11th & 12th or equivalent (not beyond), in a foreign country during the last 8
       years and must pass the qualifying examination from abroad.
 #### Reservation for Children of Indian Workers in Gulf Countries (CIWG) and its Fees Structure
 5% seats (within 15% of supernumerary quota) are reserved for Children of Indian Workers in
        Gulf (i.e., 1/3rd seats from NRI quota is reserved for CIWG). The students getting admitted
        under CIWG category shall pay tuition fee of Rs.1, 25, 000 per annum either in INR or
        equivalent USD. The students taking admissions are also informed to visit the website of the
        KLE Tech or contact directly for the same.
 Countries under CIWG are
 - United Arab Emirates
- Bahrain
- Iraq
- Iran
- Kuwait
- Oman
- Qatar an
- Saudi Arabia
 ### Essential Documents
 - Copy of the passport of the parent working in the gulf.
- Copy of Parent's visa with a validity on any date in the year 2022.
- Copy of Parent's Work Permit with a validity on any date in the year 2022.
- Certificate from the company/organization as proof that parent is working in gulf country as
        per Appendix III of UG Brochure.
- If a parent has been working in gulf till any date in the year 2022, then the certificate
        (Appendix III) may state the same.
 Eligibility of CIWG will be same as that of NRIs.
 The vacant seats if any, out of 5% seats reserved under CIWG quota shall be reverted to other
       category of DASA-2022.
 Any change in the tuition fee from competent authority from time to time will be applicable to
       CIWG also
 Notes:
 - NRI candidates who are eligible for CIWG Category will have the option to be considered for other seats
     as well. In order to avail this option, the candidates have to deposit same initial fee as applicable to
     (Non-SAARC) category candidates.
- These candidates will be shown both CIWG and all other seats as two different options for each Institute
     and branch during the academic program choice filling. They can fill their choices for both types of
     seats in order of their preferences out of all available seats under CIWG category as well as all other
     seats.
- In case, final admission is given in CIWG Category, balance amount of fee will be refunded.
- CIWG category candidates who have not opted to be considered for other seats of category, will be shown
     the seats available in CIWG category only

 Registration Fee and First Semester Tuition Fee payable to 2022 before submission of online application for
    different admitted categories are as under

| DASA (CIWG) | US $ 300/- | INR 62,500/- (or US $ 762) | US $ 1062/- | INR 87,100 |
| DASA (Non-SAARC) | US $ 300/- | US $ 4000/- | US $ 4300/- | INR 3,52,600 |

 Foreign Nationals, who are nationals of SAARC countries (except India), are eligible for 50% Tuition Fee
     waiver provided they have passed the qualifying examination in SAARC countries. They are required to pay
     an amount of US$ 2, 300 [US Dollars two thousand and three hundred only] along with the application form
     (i.e. US$ 2, 000 towards first semester tuition fee and a non- refundable registration fee of US$ 300).
 For information regarding the balance tuition fee, if any and any additional fee to be paid, please
     visit the website of the institute or contact them.
 Tuition fee for Subsequent Semesters of Study:
 The tuition fee of INR 62, 500 / US$ 4, 000 / US$ 2, 000 (as applicable) for the subsequent semesters of
     study is to be paid by the admitted applicants, directly to the Institution, as per rules of the
     Institution.
 Hostel Accommodation and Other Expenses
 Hostel fee and other expenses, which may typically range from US$ 800 - US$ 1000 per
        annum and, are required to be paid by the applicant at the time of admission. Applicants are
        advised to visit the websites or contact concerned authorities for detailed information
        regarding hostel accommodation, its availability and other expenses.
 Fee Payment
 The applicants who fulfill the admission criteria specified, should submit the application
        form online along with the requisite registration fee and first semester tuition fee. The
        fee can be paid through SWIFT /E- Payment/ NEFT/RTGS transfer. The full payable amount shall
        be paid in one transaction, for the entire payable amount.
 Any transaction charges towards SWIFT/E-Payment/NEFT/RTGS should be borne by the applicant.
