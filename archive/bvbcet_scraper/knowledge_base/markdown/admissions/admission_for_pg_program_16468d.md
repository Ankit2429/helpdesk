### Overview

Student seeking admission to M Tech/MBA/MCA for the First year shall report to Admissions
							office along with necessary order copies from CET / Comed-K / Management / PGCET.. along
							with their all other academic documents used for admissions earlier.

After data entry of each student, Fee challan shall be generated and given to the student.
							Student shall collect the challan which is to be paid in the Syndicate Bank college account
							in the campus.

Later the admission process shall continue and student shall submit the documents along with
							computer generated admission application and copy fee challan. Further student shall be
							given Provisional Admit Letter in the office which completes the admission process.


### Fee Payment

All fee payments during admission for the first year UG Programs to the University shall be made online
				only through the University portal, which shall be supported by Admission office. Alternatively one can
				make fee payment by Bank demand draft in the name of “The Registrar, KLE Technological
				University”, payable at Hubballi.

### Admission Procedure (M.Tech / MCA / MBA)

Admission to all the above PG programs, are done through the entrance exam conducted by Karnataka
				Examination Authority, Bengaluru.

Candidates seeking admission from University (management) quota need to have either PGCET
				(KEA)/CAT/MAT/GMAT rank for admissions and students need to contact the University Administrative
				Officer. Contact No.0836-2378103

Visit: kea.kar.nic.in for PGCET

Candidates are requested to visit for the admission dates on the University website: www.kletech.ac.in
