# Admission for UG Program
 **Source URL:** https://www.kletech.ac.in/hubballi/admission/domestic-admission/admission-for-ug-program
 ### Overview
 Student seeking admission to BE for the First year at KLE Technological University -
        one of the best engineering colleges in Karnataka, shall report to Admissions office along
        with necessary order copies from CET / Comed-K / Management / PGCET.. along with their all
        other academic documents used for admissions earlier.
 After data entry of each student, Fee challan shall be generated and given to the student.
        Student shall collect the challan which is to be paid in the Syndicate Bank college account
        in the campus.
 Later the admission process shall continue and student shall submit the documents along with
        computer generated admission application and copy fee challan. Further student shall be
        given Provisional Admit Letter in the office which completes the admission process.
 ### Fee Payment
 All fee payments during admission for the first year BE /B Arch Programs to the University shall be made
     online only through the University portal, which shall be supported by Admission office. Alternatively
     one can make fee payment by Bank demand draft in the name of "The Registrar, KLE Technological
     University", payable at Hubballi.

 ### Admission Procedure
 Admissions for students to any of the B.E. Programs /B.Arch. are governed by the rules and regulations
     of Karnataka Examination authority and Director of Technical Education, Bengaluru. Students willing to
     join under any of the Quota must have any one rank secured by them in CET (Karnataka) or Comed K exam or
     JEE at all India level.
 Students willing to take admission to B.Arch. have to appear for Online test conducted by Council of
     Architecture (NATA).
