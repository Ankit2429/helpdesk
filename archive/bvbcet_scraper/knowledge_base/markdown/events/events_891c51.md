PRAVRUTTI – the National Tech Fest held at Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences, Bengaluru witnessed the  exceptional display of talent and innovation from the 3rd Semester students of Department of Automation and Robotics, KLE Technological University.

In the highly competitive Robo Climb Competition, KLE Tech’s Alpha Crew team consisting of students Amogh N Betageri, Nisha N Gowda, Ananya Kumtakar, and Pavanashree Kamat clinched the first place and caught hold of the winner’s cheque of ?20,000 for their outstanding technical execution and precision.

Whilst, on the other hand, the Grind Squad, another team from KLE Tech consisting of students Nandita Nirvanashetti, Srushti Badnur, Allam Velangini Srija, and Shidhant Randewadi secured a close second, taking home a runner-up prize of ?15,000.

These stellar performances stand as examples of innovation and teamwork — a true reflection of the emerging talent driving the future of robotics in India.
