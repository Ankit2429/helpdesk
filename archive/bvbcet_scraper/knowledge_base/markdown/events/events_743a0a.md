### AICWiC

The ACM India's Celebration of Women in Computing (AICWiC) 2026, being organised at Infosys, Hubli, with KLE Technological University, Hubli as a co-host.

The ACM India's Celebration of Women in Computing (AICWiC) 2026, being organised at Infosys, Hubli, with KLE Technological University, Hubli as a co-host.

The event aims to inspire students in computing through talks by industry and academic leaders, a career-focused panel session, live demos, and presentations by all-women hackathon winner teams.

Event Details:

- Date: 24 Jan, 2026
- Venue: Infosys, Hubli
- Time: 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM (Lunch provided)
