 ### KLE Tech Hosts Pre-Summit Leadership Workshop on AI in Higher Education
 HHubballi, 3 January 2026: KLE Technological University successfully hosted the affiliated Pre-Summit Leadership Workshop titled 'The Pluriversity Model: Reimagining Higher Education in the Age of AI', bringing together top academic leaders for transformative discussions.
 Organized in collaboration with IIIT Dharwad, Jawaharlal Nehru Medical College, Belagavi, and Microsoft Research India, the workshop focused on AI-enabled transformation across key areas:
 - Institutional governance and ethics
- Faculty enablement and development
- Learning innovation and pedagogy
- Interdisciplinary collaboration
 Participants generated valuable insights and recommendations that will feed into the upcoming India AI Impact Summit 2026, highlighting the critical role of institutional partnerships in shaping AI-driven education.
 KLE Tech looks forward to continued engagement during the Summit week.
