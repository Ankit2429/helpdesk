### BVB KLE Tech Leadership Summit 2026 Brings Industry Leaders Together to Shape Future Technologies

KLE Technological University, Hubballi, in association with the BVB KLE Tech Alumni Association, successfully hosted the BVB KLE Tech Leadership Summit – 2026 at the KLE Tech Auditorium on May 23, 2026. The summit brought together distinguished industry leaders, academicians, alumni, professionals, researchers, and students for meaningful discussions on emerging technologies and future-ready innovation ecosystems.

The summit focused on three key themes — Artificial Intelligence and Automation, Semiconductor and Embedded Systems, and Global Capability Centres (GCCs). The event served as a dynamic platform for exchanging ideas on technology-driven transformation, talent development, and industry–academia collaboration.

The programme commenced with a welcome address by Ram Kerur, who highlighted the importance of alumni engagement and collaborative initiatives between academia and industry in shaping the next generation of professionals.

Delivering the inaugural address, Dr. Ashok Shettar emphasised the growing impact of Artificial Intelligence across sectors such as education, healthcare, banking, and defence. He also stressed the need to address the talent gap in the semiconductor industry in alignment with India’s evolving technological ambitions.

The keynote session on “Global Realignments: Technology, Trade and Talent” was delivered by Brahmanand Patil, who spoke about AI-enabled automotive ecosystems, software-defined mobility, intelligent simulation systems, and the importance of high-impact industry partnerships for accelerating innovation.

The summit featured multiple panel discussions led by renowned experts from leading global organisations including Samsung R&D Institute, Accenture, Cisco Systems, ATI Robotics, Bosch Global Software Technologies, Infineon Technologies, Sandisk, Lucas TVS Ltd., and Semiconductor Future Accelerator Lab Foundation.

The first panel discussion, “Physical AI: Bridging Intelligence with the Real World,” explored the ethical, ecological, economic, and psychological dimensions of AI, along with the transition from service-oriented models to product-driven innovation.

The “FinTech Fireside” session focused on technology-led financial transformation, Global Capability Centres, blockchain, cybersecurity, and the evolving fintech landscape.

Another key session on “Building India’s Semiconductor and Embedded Talent Pipeline” examined the opportunities and challenges within the semiconductor sector, highlighting the importance of policy support, industry collaboration, and academic preparedness.

An open dialogue on Industry–Academia Collaboration, chaired by Prof. P. G. Tewari, discussed collaborative pathways in research, innovation, skill development, and talent-building initiatives.

The programme concluded with a vote of thanks by Vinay Javali.

The BVB KLE Tech Leadership Summit – 2026 reaffirmed KLE Technological University’s commitment to fostering innovation, strengthening industry partnerships, and preparing students and professionals for emerging opportunities in AI, semiconductors, embedded systems, fintech, and global technology ecosystems.
