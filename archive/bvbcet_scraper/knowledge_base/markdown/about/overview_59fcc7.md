# Overview

The Central Library has a collection of 101165 Volumes of Books with 41935 Titles. The Central Library is also subscribed to 188905 ebooks from EBSCO Academic Collection with EBSCO Business Elite sources. The Central Library collects ebooks from IEEE MIT Press, IEEE Wiley, McGraw-Hill Access Engineering, Pearson Education, PHI India, New Age International, McGraw-Hill Express, Elsevier publishers, CBS, and VIDEEYA ebooks perpetual access.

The Central subscribe to 43 National and International printed Journals predominantly related to Engineering, Technology, Management, and allied subjects.

The Library subscribes to 24637 full-text E-journals from IEEE, ASME, ASCE, EBSCO Management, EBSCO Art and Architecture, and Automotive Abstract. In addition, there are 800 CDs & DVDs.

Digitization of organized collections of e-resources or publications of Faculty publications, Project Reports, Some of the rare collections of books, Question Papers, Seminar papers, and Theses are being uploaded to the Digital Library server using DSPACE is accessible within the Campus. Digitization process using CZURTEK ET 16 PLUS BOOK SCANNER being carried out.

Digital Library set up in Central Library is well facilitated with high bandwidth of 710Mbps, and the complete Campus is Wi-Fi enabled.

The Library has automated its services using the Web-Based Library Management System CONTINEO, a comprehensive package to deal with most routine library transactions. All the resources have been completely Bar Coded, which helps in Transactions, OPAC (Online Public Access of Catalogues), and reservation of Books.

The Library has facilitated remote access to e-resources through the services of the KNIMBUS Digital Library Platform with Remote Access Solution.

To promote e-learning, the Library has purchased video content management software and hosted around 23000 NPTEL Video Streaming Courses on local servers. GATE Tutor for GATE aspirants streamed across the Campus and remotely 24 x 7 uninterruptedly.

The Central Library provides a Technology Platform and Database of Plagiarism Checking Tool “Turnitin Web Tool”, GATE Tutor and “Grammarly Check” in support of R & D activities for students and professionals, also developed IRINS (Indian Research Information Network Systems) services of KLE Technological University.

The Library has RARE & Specialized collections of books, nearly 1250 with 1500 copies.

The Library has bound volumes of journals bounded since 1970. There are also 123 Architecture Design Portfolio sheets & 800 IS Standards/Data Hand Books.

The primary services are open access to resource lending of books, reference/referral service, Indian standards, SC/ST book bank scheme, POOR STUDENT book bank scheme, BRILLIANT STUDENT Book Bank Scheme, and Reprographic facilities.

The Central Library has Institutional Membership of DELNET, E-SHODHSINDU consortium, e-SHODHGANGA, CURRENT SCIENCE, NDLI National Digital Library of India, SWAYAM, and NPTEL.
