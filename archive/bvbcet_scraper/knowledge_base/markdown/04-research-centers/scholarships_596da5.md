# Scholarships at KLE Technological University

KLE Technological University (KLE Tech, formerly B. V. Bhoomaraddi College of Engineering and Technology / BVBCET) is a private university in Hubballi, Karnataka, established under the KLE Technological University Act and run by the KLE Society. As of this writing, the university's official Scholarships page (kletech.ac.in/scholarships and the linked footer page) displays only a "coming soon" placeholder, so the university has not published a university-specific scholarship list online. This document summarises what could be verified from the university's own pages, its NIRF submissions, and government scholarship portals that Karnataka students, including KLE Tech students, commonly use.

## Fee context

Scholarship amounts and tuition-fee reimbursement are best understood against the official fee structure. KLE Tech's published 2025-26 annual tuition (per the university's fee-structure page) is:

- KCET (government) quota: Rs 1,12,410 per annum for all B.E. programmes.
- COMEDK quota: approximately Rs 2,28,000 to Rs 3,04,100 per annum depending on branch.
- Management quota: up to Rs 4,00,000 per annum for Computer Science and Engineering.

Non-Karnataka students pay an additional Rs 2,500 per year; SAARC and other foreign students pay higher surcharges. [unverified: whether these figures change for later admission cycles]

## University-funded assistance

- A Careers360 profile of KLE Tech states that the university does not provide its own scholarships, but that students admitted (or seeking admission) can apply for scholarships offered by UGC, AICTE, and state and national government schemes. This is broadly consistent with the university's own NIRF filings.
- The university's NIRF 2024 submission reports the number of students receiving full tuition-fee reimbursement. For UG 4-year programmes: 368 of 4,925 students were reimbursed by State/Central Government and 30 by institution funds. For PG 2-year programmes: 17 of 316 students were reimbursed by State/Central Government. (The categories do not sum to the total student count because partial reimbursement and non-reimbursed students are not fully counted.)
- Careers360, citing NIRF data, reports 526 of 5,316 UG (4-year) students and 22 of 97 PG (2-year) students receiving full tuition-fee reimbursement in a later year.

## Government schemes students may be eligible for

The following are government schemes run by the Government of India and the Government of Karnataka. They are not university schemes, but KLE Tech students who meet the criteria may apply through the relevant portals; the university verifies applications and receives disbursements via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

### Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship (CSSS / PM-USP) - via National Scholarship Portal (NSP)

The official CSSS guidelines state that students pursuing B.Tech/B.Engg. receive Rs 12,000 per annum for the first three years and Rs 20,000 in the fourth year; postgraduate study is Rs 20,000 per annum. Eligibility: above the 80th percentile in Class XII in the relevant stream; enrolled in a regular full-time degree at an AICTE-recognised institution; gross family income up to Rs 4.5 lakh per annum; and not availing other merit scholarships or fee waivers. Applications are submitted on the NSP (scholarships.gov.in), where a One Time Registration (OTR) is required from AY 2024-25 onward. The portal is typically open from 1 June each year.

### Karnataka State Scholarship Portal (SSP)

The SSP (ssp.karnataka.gov.in) handles pre- and post-matric scholarships for students of SC, ST, OBC/backward classes, minorities, economically weaker sections, and other notified categories, across departments such as Social Welfare, Tribal Welfare, Backward Classes Welfare, and Minority Welfare. Post-matric schemes cover UG/PG courses, including professional/engineering courses. Disbursement is by DBT to Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts.

### Vidyasiri (Vidyasiri Food and Accommodation Scholarship - VFAS)

A Karnataka Backward Classes Welfare Department scheme providing a monthly living allowance of Rs 1,500 for 10 months (Rs 15,000 per year) to backward-class students in post-matric courses, including engineering, who are not accommodated in government hostels. Eligibility includes Karnataka domicile, a notified OBC category, prescribed income limits, and at least 75% attendance. Applications are submitted through the SSP post-matric portal.

### Post-Matric Scholarship for Minorities

The Karnataka Directorate of Minorities awards post-matric scholarships to minority-community students from Class XI through UG, PG, M.Phil and PhD. Applications are submitted through SSP with e-attestation of documents; the page does not publish amounts.

## Student-reported observations

Student reviews aggregated on GetMyUni note that state (SSP) and central (NSP) scholarships are widely received by KLE Tech students. [unverified: frequency and coverage]

## Guidance

- Register on both the NSP (scholarships.gov.in) and Karnataka SSP portals, keep an Aadhaar-seeded bank account active, and apply within the published deadlines.
- Contact the university's help desk (0836-2378103/105/106, info@kletech.ac.in) for confirmation on how government scholarships are verified and disbursed.
