# News
## Jun 27, 2026
### KLE-CTiE Successfully Hosts MedihealthXPO 2026 and North Karnataka Incubators Consortium
The KLE Centre for Technology Innovation & Entrepreneurship (KLE-CTiE), KLE Technological University, Hubballi, successfully organised MedihealthXPO 2026 – MedTech Innovation Showcase & North Karnataka Incubators Consortium on June 27, 2026, at the CTIE Tech Park Building and Old Senate Hall.
The event was inaugurated by Dr. Prakash G. Tewari, Hon. Vice Chancellor, KLE Technological University, and Dr. Anant Koppar. Dr. Vinayak Hosamani, Director, KLE-CTiE; Mr. Harish Agadi, Associate Director; and Mr. Sanjay Narendra, KLE-CTiE, were also present on the occasion.
The event witnessed participation from representatives of IIT Dharwad, KLE KAHER, KDEM, IIIT Research Park, Jain College Belagavi, UAS Dharwad, and VTU Belagavi, along with startup founders, healthcare professionals, academicians, investors, and incubation managers from across North Karnataka.
MedTech Innovation Showcase
MedihealthXPO 2026 showcased innovative MedTech and HealthTech solutions developed by startups incubated at KLE-CTiE through live product demonstrations and startup exhibits. The event provided a valuable platform for innovators to interact with healthcare professionals, industry experts, investors, and researchers, fostering collaboration and commercialisation opportunities.
North Karnataka Incubators Consortium
A key highlight of the programme was the North Karnataka Incubators Consortium, which brought together incubation centres from across the HDB region to discuss collaborative initiatives, share best practices, and strengthen the regional startup ecosystem.
The event concluded with startup pitch presentations, networking sessions, and discussions on advancing innovation, reaffirming KLE-CTiE’s commitment to promoting technology-driven entrepreneurship and supporting the growth of innovative startups in the region.
