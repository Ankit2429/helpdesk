# Center for Technology Innovation and Entrepreneurship (CTIE)

## Overview & Government Recognition
The Centre for Technology Innovation and Entrepreneurship (CTIE) at KLE Technological University is a Technology Business Incubator (TBI) recognized and sponsored by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India, and supported under the MeitY TIDE 2.0 Incubator scheme.

## Incubation Infrastructure & Impact
- **Incubation Space:** Offers over 18,000 sq. ft. of dedicated incubation space and tech park facility on the Vidya Nagar Campus.
- **Startups Incubated:** CTIE has incubated over 135+ startups across technology, IoT, MedTech, agriculture, and engineering domains.
- **Job Creation:** Startups incubated at CTIE have generated over 2,000+ jobs in the region.
- **Services Provided:** Offers incubation space, prototype development labs, technical mentorship, IP facilitation, seed-funding guidance, and industry networking.

## Application & Admission for Startups
Early-stage startups and student entrepreneurs seeking incubation space, technical guidance, or seed grant support can apply directly to CTIE through the KLE Tech innovation portal.
