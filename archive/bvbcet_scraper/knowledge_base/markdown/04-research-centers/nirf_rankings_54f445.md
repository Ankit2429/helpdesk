# NIRF Rankings

KLE Technological University (KLE Tech), Hubballi, Karnataka, participates in the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) of the Ministry of Education, Government of India. This note summarises the university's NIRF participation, its most recent data submissions, and the ranks that could be verified from the official NIRF portal as of August 2026.

## Participation and Latest Submission (NIRF 2026)

KLE Tech submitted institute data for NIRF 2026 in all three categories it participates in:

- Engineering (Institute ID IR-E-U-0854) - submitted 2 February 2026
- Overall (Institute ID IR-O-U-0854) - submitted 7 February 2026
- Innovation (Institute ID IR-I-U-0854) - submitted 12 February 2026

These submissions (captured in the university's own corpus documents) report the data below but do not state any rank. **No NIRF 2026 result for KLE Tech is published on the official NIRF portal as of August 2026, so the 2026 rank is not confirmed.**

## Verified NIRF Ranks (Engineering)

From the official NIRF portal (nirfindia.org), KLE Tech's Engineering ranking placement:

- **NIRF 2024 (Engineering): rank band 151-200.** Confirmed on the official NIRF 2024 Engineering rank-band page; the institution is not in the 1-100 or 101-150 bands. Band placements carry no individual rank number or score.
- **NIRF 2025 (Engineering): rank band 151-200.** Confirmed on the official NIRF 2025 Engineering rank-band page; not in the 1-100 or 101-150 bands.
- NIRF 2023 (Engineering): not found in the 1-100, 101-150, or 151-200 lists on the NIRF portal.

No official NIRF score has been published for KLE Tech in any year; ranked-band listings show the institution name but not a numeric score.

## Other Categories - Not Ranked in Recent Years

- **Innovation:** KLE Tech appears on the NIRF 2024 Innovation "List of Participating Institutions," confirming participation, but it is not ranked in NIRF 2024 or NIRF 2025 Innovation (not in the top 10 or the 11-50 band in either year).
- **Overall:** Not found in the NIRF 2024 or NIRF 2025 Overall rankings (not in the 1-100, 101-150, or 151-200 lists in either year).

## Data Submitted in the NIRF 2026 Submissions

Engineering submission (key figures):

- Sanctioned UG (4-year) intake: 1,380 (AY 2024-25); PG (2-year): 90.
- Student strength: UG 4-year 5,316 (3,255 male, 2,061 female); PG 97.
- UG placements: 754 of 1,239 graduates placed in 2024-25 (median salary Rs 7,20,000); 890 of 1,249 in 2022-23 and 712 of 1,169 in 2023-24 (median Rs 7,00,000).
- Ph.D: 29 full-time and 145 part-time scholars; 4 full-time and 20 part-time graduated in 2024-25.
- Patents granted: 9 (2024), 5 (2023), 1 (2022).
- Sponsored research: 6 projects (Rs 14.5 lakh) in 2024-25; consultancy: 21 projects (Rs 2.00 crore) in 2024-25.

Overall submission additionally reports UG 3-year (1,309), UG 5-year (241), PG (342) and PG-integrated (60) students, and a valid NAAC accreditation (CGPA 3.10, valid 15-03-2022 to 14-03-2027). The Innovation submission reports 30+ DPIIT-recognised startups, 9 patents granted in 2024, one patent commercialised per year (2022-2024), and government innovation grants including MEITY TIDE 2.0 (Rs 1.00 crore in 2024-25).
