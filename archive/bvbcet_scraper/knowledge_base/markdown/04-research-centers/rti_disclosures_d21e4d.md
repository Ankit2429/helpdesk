# RTI and Mandatory Disclosures

## What the RTI process requires

Under the Right to Information Act, 2005, public authorities must proactively publish disclosures and must answer RTI applications. For a university, the key offices are the Public Information Officer (PIO) / Central Public Information Officer (CPIO), who receives applications, and the First Appellate Authority, who hears appeals. Applications are filed in writing or online with a prescribed fee.

## RTI at KLE Technological University

KLE Technological University (KLE Tech), Hubballi, publishes a dedicated RTI page at https://www.kletech.ac.in/about/recognitions-approvals/rti.

The university's "Public Self Disclosure" document lists, under "Information Corner," a mandatory entry titled "RTI: Details of Central Public Information Officer (CPIO) and Appellate Authority (wherever applicable)" pointing to the RTI page URL above, but names neither officer.

**RTI officer contact details are not published in the available knowledge-base documents.** The RTI page could not be fetched during research (HTTP 403 Forbidden), and no independent source listing the KLE Tech PIO/CPIO or First Appellate Authority was found. No officer name, contact, or application-fee amount is stated here; applicants should check the RTI page above. [unverified]

## What mandatory disclosures normally contain

AICTE and UGC require institutions to publish: program-wise fee structure and refund policy; sanctioned student intake and admission process; faculty details; infrastructure (land, buildings, classrooms, labs, library, ICT); governance and finances (statutes, budgets, audit reports); academic results and exam schedules; and placements and student support (placement records, scholarships, hostels, grievance cells).
