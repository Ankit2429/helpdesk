# Placements and Leading Recruiters

KLE Technological University, Hubballi (Karnataka), formerly BVBCET, publishes placement statistics in its own placement brochures. The figures below come from those brochures and the university's "About Placements" web page.

## Placement Highlights

- Roughly 100+ named core recruiters across semiconductors/VLSI, automotive, software services, and consulting, with batch-wise top-recruiter tables covering 2022 through 2026 (ongoing).
- Highest packages: Twilio at 43 LPA (2022, 2 students) and Amazon SDE at 46.38 Lakh CTC (2024, 1 student). Robert Bosch awarded the university for Maximum Campus Selections (100+ students) in the 2018-19 batch.

## Training and Placement Cell

- **Soft skills training "VIKAS":** 64 hours over 4 semesters by a reputed Bengaluru organization. Modules: Analytical Thinking, Mathematical Thinking, Creative + Lateral Thinking, Technical Thinking, Corporate Behavior, Written English, Spoken English.
- **Campus incubation:** around 38 companies (many start-ups) operate from campus, offering internships and real-world problem statements for student projects.
- **Contact:** Prof. C. D. Kerure, Training & Placement Officer, KLE Technological University, BVB Campus, Vidyanagar, Hubballi-580031, Karnataka. Mobile: 98451 17196; Email: placement@kletech.ac.in.

## Notes on Data Reliability

- All recruiter names and batch counts come from KLE Tech's own placement brochures and are reported exactly as printed; the brochures give no average package figures, so none are stated here.
- Package figures labeled [unverified] appear on the university's "About Placements" web page without a clear batch-year mapping and were not independently cross-checked.
