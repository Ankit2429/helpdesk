 # Centre for Artificial Intelligence Research (CAIR)
 The Centre for AI Research (CAIR) at KLE Technological University is committed to
 building a thriving research community focused on developing intelligent solutions leveraging Edge
 AI, High-Performance Computing, Data Science, and Cloud Technologies. CAIR aims to create an
 ecosystem that drives research and innovation in these essential fields and also collaborates with
 industries and established research organizations to arrive at real-time solutions.
