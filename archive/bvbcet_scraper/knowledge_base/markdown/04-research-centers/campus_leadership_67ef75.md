# Campus Leadership and Administration

## Who heads the Hubballi campus?

KLE Technological University (KLE Tech), Hubballi is a State Private University, not a
degree college, so it has **no "Principal"**. The head of the university and of the
B. V. Bhoomaraddi Campus, Hubballi is the **Vice-Chancellor**, supported by the
Pro-Chancellor and the Chancellor. (The campus was previously B. V. Bhoomaraddi College of
Engineering and Technology, BVBCET, which was headed by a Principal until the institution
became a university.)

- **Chancellor** (Head of the University): **Dr. Prabhakar B. Kore** — also Chairman, Board
  of Management, KLE Society, Belagavi. The Chancellor is appointed by the sponsoring body
  (KLE Society) and his decision is final and binding on disputes.
- **Pro-Chancellor** (assists the Chancellor, acts in his absence): **Dr. Ashok S. Shettar** —
  a former Vice-Chancellor of KLE Tech.
- **Vice-Chancellor** (Principal Executive and Academic Officer; the de-facto head of the
  campus): **Dr. Prakash G. Tewari**.
- **Registrar** (Principal Administrative Officer, Member Secretary of the Board of Governors):
  **Dr. Basavaraj S. Anami**.
