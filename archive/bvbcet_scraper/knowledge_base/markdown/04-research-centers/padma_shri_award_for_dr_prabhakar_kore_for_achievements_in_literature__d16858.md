# News
## Jan 28, 2026
### Padma Shri Award for Dr. Prabhakar Kore for Achievements in Literature, Education, and Promotion of Globally Recognised Health Research.
The conferment of the prestigious Padma Shri award by the Government of India on Dr. Prabhakar Kore, who has gained national recognition for his exceptional service and visionary leadership in the fields of literature, education, and promotion of globally recognised health research, is a matter of great pride for the people of Karnataka.
Believing education to be a powerful instrument of social transformation, Dr. Prabhakar Kore has played a significant role in delivering quality education to students from rural and backward regions. Through the KLE Society, he has established numerous educational institutions in the fields of medicine, advanced and globally recognised medical and health research, technology, engineering, and general education, thereby illuminating the lives of lakhs of students and contributing to advancements in healthcare.
In addition to education, he has made notable contributions to the promotion of literature and cultural activities. His role in imparting the values of ethics, culture, and humanism to the younger generation through value-based education is highly commendable. His vision—that education should not be confined merely to employment but should also contribute to social development, nation-building, and healthcare advancement—has earned widespread appreciation.
The Padma Shri award conferred in recognition of Dr. Prabhakar Kore’s achievements stands as an honour to his lifelong service and is expected to inspire future generations. Members of the entire KLE family and other eminent personalities have extended their heartfelt congratulations on this distinguished achievement.
