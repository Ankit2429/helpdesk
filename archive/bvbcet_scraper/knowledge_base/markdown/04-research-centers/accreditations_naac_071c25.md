# Accreditations and Approvals

KLE Technological University (KLE Tech), Hubballi, Karnataka, is a State Private University established under the KLE Technological University Act, 2012 (Karnataka Act No. 22 of 2013). It traces its roots to B. V. Bhoomaraddi College of Engineering and Technology (BVBCET), founded by the KLE Society in 1947, which gained academic autonomy from the University Grants Commission (UGC) in 2007. The university holds approvals and accreditations from the UGC, AICTE, NAAC, NBA, and participated in the World Bank-supported TEQIP program.

## Establishment: KLE Technological University Act, 2012

- The university was established by the **KLE Technological University Act, 2012 (Karnataka Act No. 22 of 2013)**; its Statutes and Regulations are framed under this Act and approved by the Board of Governors.
- The university formally came into existence on **4 April 2015**, transforming the erstwhile BVB College of Engineering and Technology into a university. The university's mandatory disclosure states that "in 2014 the college was recognized as a state private University by Government of Karnataka."
- Para 6 of the Act empowers the university, after completing five years of existence, to establish constituent colleges, additional campuses, regional centres and study centres, with prior approval of the State Government and subject to UGC and national accreditation norms. Under this provision, the university took KLE Dr. M. S. Sheshgiri College of Engineering and Technology, Belagavi, and KLE Society's Law College, Bengaluru, as constituent units, giving three campuses (Hubballi, Belagavi, Bengaluru).

## NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council)

- In the **first cycle** of NAAC accreditation, the university received an **'A' grade with a CGPA of 3.10**, declared on **15 March 2022** and valid until **14 March 2027**, per the university's IQAC minutes (16 May 2022).
- A NAAC peer team visited the university on **7-9 March 2022** for university assessment.
- This is consistent with the UGC 12(B) letter of November 2021, which advised the university to obtain NAAC accreditation "at the earliest." Placement brochure and admissions pages also state "NAAC accredited ('A' Grade)."
- The university continues its NAAC cycle obligations through Annual Quality Assurance Reports (AQAR 2021-22 resubmitted August 2023; AQAR 2022-23), both present in the corpus.

## AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education) Approval

- As a technical university, KLE Tech seeks AICTE approval for its technical/engineering programs. Board of Governors minutes record that the application for AICTE approval for all technical programmes was submitted on **3 February 2018**, followed by document verification at AICTE New Delhi (17 and 24 February 2018) and an AICTE Expert Verification Committee visit on **2 March 2018**.
- The university's AICTE recognition pages list AICTE approval letters (Parts One and Two) for 2018-19 and 2019-20, along with Board of Governors and IQAC meeting minutes.

## NBA (National Board of Accreditation)

- The following programmes are NBA-accredited (per the university's AICTE accreditation-status pages and the 2019-20 annual report): **Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, and Biotechnology** - with the AICTE pages noting "Accredited with 'A' Grade."
- Board of Governors minutes (13th and 14th meetings) state that "four of its engineering programmes are accredited by NBA with more than 675 marks," which the university cited to meet Karnataka State Higher Education Council eligibility norms (at least three programmes with a minimum NBA score of 675).
- The corpus also contains NBA Tier-I/Tier-II Data Capturing Point reports (e.g., UG Computer Science & Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, Biotechnology), indicating ongoing NBA processes.
- Note: The AICTE accreditation-status table lists "Electrical and Electronics Engineering" and "Automation and Robotics" among programmes not applied for NBA and, inconsistently in the source, also lists Biotechnology under "not applied" even though it appears as accredited.

## UGC 2(f) and 12(B) Status

- **12(B):** A UGC Speed Post letter (No. F.8-28/2015(CPP-I/PU), November 2021) states that KLE Technological University has been included in the **list of universities under Section 12(B) of the UGC Act, 1956**. This followed a UGC Expert Committee visit on 21-22 August 2021 and ratification at the Commission's 553rd meeting (26 October 2021), subject to the condition that financial assistance under all UGC schemes is limited to those relating to teachers and students only.
- **2(f):** The university's website lists a "University Grants Commission - 2F Approval" page under "Recognitions & Approvals," and its Public Self Disclosure references a "2f-approval" page. However, the corpus copy of that page is only a navigation stub with no letter number or date, so the details of the 2(f) inclusion are not verifiable from the available documents. [unverified] - UGC 2(f) recognition is listed by the university, but the specific order/date could not be confirmed.

## TEQIP (Technical Education Quality Improvement Programme)

- The institution participated in **TEQIP-III** (a World Bank and Government of India program), launched April 2017, with a total grant allocation of about Rs. 7 crore (Rs. 847 lakhs per BOG minutes; Rs. 823.38 lakhs spent; project closing 31 March 2021). BVBCET served as mentor institute for Government Engineering College, Ramgarh, Jharkhand.
- The institution received the **TEQIP "Good Governance Award."** Earlier TEQIP-II (World Bank) grants are also recorded in the annual reports.

## Rankings (for context)

- NIRF: among the top 200 institutions in NIRF 2022; a 151-200 band in the University category (placement brochure); 151-200 among engineering colleges in NIRF 2024 (per Wikipedia). Also ranked among India's top 25 private universities under the ATAL-2020 ranking by AICTE/MHRD.
