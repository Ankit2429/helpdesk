# B.B.A. LL.B
 **Source URL:** https://www.kletech.ac.in/course/bba-llb
 ## The 5-year B.B.A.LL.B. integrated programme commenced from 2012 and combines Bachelors' degree program in Business Administration together with the Bachelors' Degree program in Law.
 With a successful track record of 11 graduating cohorts, this program has consistently proven to be the ideal choice for individuals who are keen on making a commitment to the legal profession early in their educational journey, right after 10+2 stage.
 The programme offers Management courses along with the Law courses to cater to the needs of aspiring lawyers who would like to seek placements in the corporate sector or take to being a CS.

 #### Eligibility Criteria

 3. Minimum 40% aggregate marks in the qualifying examination for SC/ST candidates.
 4. Admission is subject to the rules and regulations of the Bar Council of India (BCI), KLE Technological University, Hubballi and the Institution.
