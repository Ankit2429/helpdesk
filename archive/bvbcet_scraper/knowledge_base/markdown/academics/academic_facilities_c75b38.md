### Classrooms

In terms of infrastructural prowess, KLE Tech University has classroom facilities as per international standards to facilitate the new-age teaching-learning process.

Each classroom has all facilities in place to understand and address all the specific needs of the students studying across streams and disciplines. The university believes that higher learning is quite untenable without proper, functional classrooms.

Therefore, all the departments have perfectly ventilated, amply spacious, and well-laid-out classrooms furnished with comfortable seating arrangements.

KLE Tech University has a well-equipped, world-class chain of laboratories to provide students with an exhaustive practical experience across streams and disciplines.

All labs have all the required tools, equipment, and allied resources to support students in carrying out practical sessions to put theoretical aspects into practice. The labs provide students with an opportunity to apply their knowledge concerning all specific streams of study. KLE Tech University has facilitated the extensive development of different world-class laboratories across departments intending to match the overall quality of the university's infrastructure with that of global educational establishments.

The University has various computer labs backed by richly configured high-performance computers complemented by high-speed broadband connectivity. All computers have all the relevant software to support the students in the new-age learning process.

The University has established various research laboratories intending to produce high-quality research across streams and disciplines. Some of the top research facilities include advanced computation, advanced project development, modern labs for fire testing, composite testing, and advanced construction materials.

The central library at KLE Tech University is equipped with state-of-the-art infrastructure on a carpet area of 3000 square meters with a seating capacity of more than 600 learners and scholars.
