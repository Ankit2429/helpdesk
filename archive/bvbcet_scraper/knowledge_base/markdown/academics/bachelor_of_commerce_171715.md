## The Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) opens up careers in accounting, business, economics, finance and management.

KLE Tech offers a learning environment that stimulates, challenges and fulfils the potential of ambitious students, leading to personal development, and meaningful careers.

The three/four years’ program will prepare you with skills and knowledge in Accounting, Finance, economics, auditing, corporate tax, business management areas. Through experiential learning and authentic assessment tasks, you will develop practical, real-world skills to adapt and add value in today’s dynamic and competitive business environment. The coursework is complemented with interventions to sharpen or develop soft skills like communication, teamwork, interpersonal skills, and critical thinking, which are essential for a successful career.
