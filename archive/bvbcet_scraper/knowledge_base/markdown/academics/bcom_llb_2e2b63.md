# B.Com. LL.B
 **Source URL:** https://www.kletech.ac.in/course-detail/b-com-ll-b/28
 ## The B.Com. LL.B. is a 5-year integrated programme that combines Bachelors' degree programme in Commerce together with the  Bachelors' Degree programme in Law.
 With a successful track record of 6 graduating cohorts, this programme has consistently proven to be the ideal choice for individuals who are keen on making a commitment to the legal profession early in their educational journey, right after 10+2 stage.
 The programme offers a unique blend of commerce courses along with the law courses and enables the law practitioners to be placed in business and management firms that require legal advice in money matters or take to being a CA/CS
