Exciting Inauguration Ceremony - November 18th, 9:15 AM
	Venue: Auditorium, KLE Law College, Bengaluru
	Chief Guest
	- Hon’ble Mr. Justice T. G. Shivashankare Gowda, Judge, High Court of Karnataka

Guests of Honour:
	- Mr. Venkatesh Murthy K., Senior Director, Data Security Council of India
	- Mr. Govinda Mengji, Cyber Security & Technology Manager, Deloitte, Abu Dhabi, UAE
	- Prof. (Dr.) Andrew Koh, Senior Lecturer of Computer Science, Singapore Management University, Singapore

President: Prof. (Dr.) Ashok Shettar, Vice Chancellor, KLE Technological University, Hubballi

Insightful Plenary Sessions and Panel Discussions - November 18th, 1:45 PM

Venue: Auditorium, KLE Law College, Bengaluru

-Chairs:Prof. (Dr.) M. K. Bhandari and Mr. Na.Vijaya Shankar

Resource Persons: Prof. (Dr.) Andrew Koh, Mr. Govinda Mengji, Prof. (Dr.) Ananth Prabhu G, Prof. (Dr.) K.V.S Hari, Mr. M.G. Kodandaram, Dr. M.N. Bheemesh

Panel Discussion - November 18th, 1:30 PM
	Venue: Auditorium, KLE Law College, Bengaluru
	- Chair:Dr. Raghu Prasad
	- Resource Persons Mr. Arun Soni, Mr. Tony Moroney, Dr. Josina Rodrigues
	- Moderator Mr. Na.Vijaya Shankar
	- Panelists Dr. Raghu Prasad, Mr. Pushpendra K Dixit, Mr. Saakshar Duggal, Mr. Alkali Amana, Mr. Amit Dubey

Grand Valedictory Ceremony - November 19th, 1:30 PM
	- Venue: Auditorium, KLE Law College, Bengaluru
	- Chief Guest: Hon’ble Mr. Justice Suraj Govindaraj, Judge, High Court of Karnataka
	- Guests of Honour:
	- Dr. Pavan Duggal, Advocate, Supreme Court of India
	- Mr. Amit Dubey, Advisor (Cybersecurity), Economic Council of India
	- Prof. (Dr.) Nishtha Jaswal, Vice Chancellor, Himachal Pradesh National Law University, Shimla
	- Mr. Na. Vijayashankar, Data Protection and Data Governance Consultant, Certified Independent Director, Bengaluru
