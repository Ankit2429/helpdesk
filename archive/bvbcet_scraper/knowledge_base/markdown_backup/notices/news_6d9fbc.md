### Samsung Chip Design Studio Contest

Students from KLE Technological University’s B E Electronics Engineering, VLSI Design & Technology, secured 1st place in Samsung Chip Design Studio contest.

The name of the participants who took part in this event to make KLE Tech proud are mentioned hereby:

- Shashidhar kumbar 8th sem
- Pratiksha kulkarni 8th sem
- Navneet Patil, th sem
- Tanush Kavalikatti, 4th sem
- Manasvi Chavaragi, 4th sem

KLE Technological University congratulates the students and appreciates their efforts in achieving such a milestone.
