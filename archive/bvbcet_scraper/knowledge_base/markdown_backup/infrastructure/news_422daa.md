## Jan 04, 2024


### KLE Tech and Dassault Systemes collaborate for Centre of Excellence

In a monumental stride towards embracing the transformative era of Industry 4.0, KLE Technological University proudly announces its membership in the esteemed '3D EXPERIENCE Center of Excellence'. The agreement, formalized during the ICTIEE-2024, marks a pivotal partnership between KLE Technological University and Dassault Systemes.

During the ICTIEE-2024 event, an agreement was inked between KLE Technological University and Dassault Systemes, cementing this significant affiliation. Mr. Xavier Fouger, Senior Director of Learning Centers and Programs at Dassault Systemes, presented a member plaque to Dr. Ashok Shettar, Vice Chancellor of KLE Technological University, signifying this momentous collaboration.

The inclusion of KLE Technological University within the Global Edu Center of Excellence fraternity places it amongst a prestigious cohort of institutions globally, including Monterrey Tec, USC Viterbi, Cranfield University, Illinois Tech, and others.

By affiliating with this initiative, KLE Technological University aims to synergize its strengths with the resources of the Edu Center of Excellence Partnership. This collaboration is poised to create a holistic learning environment, fostering an enriched educational experience for the educator community in the areas of smart manufacturing, digital twins, automation and robotics.

The '3D EXPERIENCE Edu Centers of Excellence' are physical entities, strategically managed by select Dassault Systèmes partners. These centers amalgamate industry-specific 3DEXPERIENCE knowledge with their own expertise, delivering comprehensive educational programs for learners seeking expertise in industry 4.0 related technologies. These centers serve as immersive hubs for students and educators to delve into industry-relevant skills required in the digital age.

The primary objective of these Centers of Excellence is to expedite experiential lifelong learning within local ecosystems. They provide a nexus for businesses and governments to access centers facilitating the development of expertise crucial for the industry's virtual collaborative leap and the closing of the skills gap. Dassault Systèmes' certification program plays a pivotal role in validating the skills and knowledge acquired through these centers.

KLE Technological University's affiliation with the '3D EXPERIENCE Center of Excellence' is a testament to its commitment to delivering world-class education and staying at the forefront of technological advancements in the digital landscape.
