## K.L.E. SOCIETY'S LAW COLLEGE

(Affiliated to Karnataka State Law University, Hubballi, Recognized by BCI and Govt. of Karnataka)

## NOTICE

This is to inform all parents and students that in view of recent (29th January

2022) SOP issued by the Govt of Karnataka, the OFFLINE classes shall commence for all

classes from 1st February 2022. All are informed to attend the classes accordingly. Also,

note the following:

1. Considering the travel time required for those who have returned home and to ensure no loss of classes for such students, the classes shall be held in hybrid mode (online and offline) ONLY till 5th February 2022.
2. Online classes will stop, and only offline classes shall continue from 7th February 2022. Please make your arrangements and attend classes accordingly.
3. Covid appropriate behaviour shall be always adhered strictly by observing the necessary protocols prescribed by the college and the Govt.
4. Offline classes shall be held as per the timetable already in place and published to you.
5. Please get vaccinated, if not done so far and keep the class teacher informed anytime you're found symptomatic, for the safety of all.

Date: 30-01-2022

Place: Bengaluru

## GOVERNMENT OF KARNATAKA

No. RD 158 TNR 2020

Karnataka Government Secretariat, Vidhana Soudha, Bengaluru, Dated:29-01-2022

## ORDER

Whereas, the State Government has imposed graded containment measures videorders of even number dated 04-01-2022, 11-01-2022, 21-012022 &amp; its connecting orders to combat the resurge of COVID 19 in the State driven mainly by Omicron variant of concern, which will be in force upto 31-01-2022.

Whereas, the prevailing COVID 19 in the State was reviewed on 29-012022. It is observed that there has been a steady decline in new COVID19 cases in the State. Further, 97% of the active cases are in home isolation and only 3% of active cases are requiring hospitalization. The Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) has recommended opening up of more activities on assessing current situation. Further, TAC has suggested the need to have continued focus on the five-foldstrategy of Test-Track-Treat-Vaccination andAdherence to COVID-appropriate behaviour to break the chain of transmission of COVID 19 to sustain the gains made and to further reduce the case load in the State.

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred under the Section 24 (1) of the Disaster Management Act, 2005, the undersigned, in the capacity of the Chairman, State Executive Committee, hereby issued modifications to the containment measures issued vide orders of even number dated 04-01-2022, 1101-2022,21-01-2022 &amp; its connecting orders, as below, which will come into force from 31-01-2022, and will remain in force upto 15-02-2022 for strict implementation by Chief Commissioner BBMP, Police Commissioners, Deputy Commissioners, Superintendents of Police, and other Heads of Departments and Authorities.

1. The night curfew imposed from 10.00 pm to 5.00 am, of nextday, in the State is withdrawn, with effect from 31-01-2022.
2. In Bengaluru Urban district, all Schools and College permitted to Re-open from 31-01-2022 strictly adhering to COVID-19 Appropriate Behaviour and following SOPs issued by the concerned Department. Schools and colleges shall function as per the assessment of the respective District Administration /BBMP.
3. Pubs / Clubs / Restaurants / Bars / Hotels / eating places in Hotel etc., permitted to operate with upto100% of its seating capacity strictly adhering to COVID Appropriate Behaviour and entry to such places will be restricted to fully vaccinated persons only.

5. Marriage functions are permitted to be conducted involving not more than 300 people in open spaces and 200 people in closed places strictly adhering to COVID 19 Appropriate Behaviour and prevailing guidelines issued by State Government.
