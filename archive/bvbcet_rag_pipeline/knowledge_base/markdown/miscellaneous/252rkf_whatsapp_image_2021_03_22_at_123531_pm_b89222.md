## INSTRUCTIONS FOR STUDENTS ATTENDING MARCH/APRIL2021 EXAMINATION

Date: 22d March 2021

With view to ensure health safety and security of all and to comply with SOP's issued by the governments periodically. all are informed to observe the following instruetions without fail.

- r Entry into the campus shall be 30 minutes before the scheduled time of examination and students shall leave the campus immediately after the examination without flocking inside and/or outside the campus.
- All the students shall get the thermal scan checked at the entry to the campus and shall strietly use sanitizers, face masks (not to pull the masks below the nose anytime) and maintain distance at all the time during their presence in the campus.
- All the students shall wear ID Cards compulsorily.
- Students are instructed to wash their hands before and after their examination.
- Y Students are advised to use face shield.
- Students are instructed to bring their own water boutles and food from home and sharing of the same is strietly prohibited. However, potable water is made available at the college.
- Students having tested positive for COVID-19 and/or having symptoms like cold, cough or fever are strictly instructed to report to the Examination In-Charge Dr. Mahesh R. S. for suitable seating arrangement to be made.
- All Students are required to install 'ArogvaSert' App from the Google Play store.
- Any violation of safety measures as preseribed and to be informed from time to time may result in penalty and denial of entry to examination hall.
