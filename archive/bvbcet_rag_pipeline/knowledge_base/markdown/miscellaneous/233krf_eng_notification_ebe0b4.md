## NOTIFICATION

The date for filling up of Annual convocation application of Karnataka State Law University is extended up to 08-02-2022 in view of the announcement of Revaluation / Challenge Valuation results of June-2021 examination. Attested copy of the statement of marks of all the semesters should be enclosed with the filled convocation application form.
