## CIRCULAR

Sub: Postponement of Examination

Ref: Hon'ble Vice-Chancellor approval dated: 11-02- 202

In view of the judgment of the Hon'ble High Court of Karnataka in the Writ Petition No.14389 of 2020 ( EDN-RES) A/W Write Petition Nos.2197 of 2021, 1850/2021 (EDN-RES), dated 8th Day of February, 2021, the examinations notified by the Karnataka State Law University, Hubballi, scheduled from 15-02-2021 to 08-03-2021 have been postponed. The colleges are instructed to bring this circular to all the concerned students.
