## Dear Parent and Student,

This is to share with you that I am aware of the concerns and the anguish that is continuously being expressed from the students and parents regarding the academics at KSLU. In view of prolonged academic calendar due to COVID times and also due to some students approaching the political leaders and courts for postponement of exams, unfortunately the academics remain disturbed, and I personally feel which is uncalled for. However, I shall inform that, KSLU circulars provide for promotion (NOT pass) of all students to the higher class, thus, the admissions to the next class is being announced by the University, last date being Nov 20 (without fine) / Nov 25 (with late fine of Rs 1000/-).

All my colleagues and I do receive several queries which are genuine under the circumstances regarding payment of admission fees without having neither exams nor commencement of classes. While I find the concerns to be fair and genuine, yet I wish to advise that, availing the facility of admission to higher class and saving possible timeline in your academics is the wise thing to do. Hence, advising you to get admitted to the next class as per the schedule above to avoid loss of year.

As the things are unclear, let me also predict and suggest to you that in view of happenings at the courts, the stay that is granted recently for the exams may be vacated and exams may start soon. The court may also suggest a formula in place of examinations, then the matter will be handled accordingly. If stay is not vacated and the matter is not decided soon, the University may start classes, pending the exams and sometime pause classes to conduct exams and resume.
